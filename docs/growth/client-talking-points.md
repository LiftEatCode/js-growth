# Client-Safe Growth Methodology Talking Points

Purpose: explain JS Solutions measurement expertise professionally **without** exposing proprietary audit scoring, prompts, commercial mappings, competitive algorithms, or security-sensitive internals.

---

## Measurement first

- “We establish a measurement baseline before changing strategy so we can separate real improvement from normal traffic fluctuations.”
- “Growth work is treated as measurable experiments with a hypothesis, KPI, baseline, and review date.”

## Acquisition vs conversion

- “We track acquisition separately from conversion because more traffic is not useful if it does not produce meaningful customer actions.”
- “We prioritize qualified traffic — visitors who show intent — over raw session volume.”

## Attribution

- “We use campaign attribution to identify which marketing channels actually contribute to website engagement and leads.”
- “We keep paid and organic social tagging consistent so Facebook and other channels are comparable over time.”

## SEO & local

- “Search visibility metrics like impressions help us understand opportunity, but we judge success by qualified clicks, landing-page engagement, and downstream leads.”
- “Local and service-intent queries are reviewed separately from brand searches when the data supports it.”

## Funnel clarity

- “We map the customer journey from first visit through audit usage and contact actions, then into our private commercial pipeline.”
- “Marketing analytics observes engagement; payment and contract systems remain the authority for revenue.”

## Privacy & trust

- “We do not put customer personal information or internal commercial record identifiers into public analytics tools.”
- “Clients can expect clear reporting on what moved — without unnecessary technical noise.”

## What not to say

Do **not** disclose:

- Proprietary Website Growth Score rules or weights
- Internal AI prompts or model routing
- Commercial pricing/mapping internals
- Exact competitive scoring algorithms
- Internal automation architecture or credentials
- Security/session implementation details
