# GROWTH-EXP-2026-010 — Photo vs text educational content

**Status:** READY  
**Publisher:** COMPANY (primary); optional FOUNDER mirror with separate ledger  
**Primary job:** AUTHORITY  
**Formats:** PHOTO vs TEXT

## Hypothesis

For the same educational topic, photo posts will produce higher reach/views than text-only posts on the JS Solutions Page — without harming engagement rate.

## Primary metric

Content views (or reach if views NOT_CAPTURED)

## Secondary metrics

Engagements, engagement rate, Page visits, followers gained (if attributable), Facebook-attributed audit starts

## Method

1. Pick one topic (e.g. local SEO mistake).  
2. Publish PHOTO version week A; TEXT version week B (or alternate topics matched by pillar).  
3. Record both in GrowthContentRecord.  
4. Capture Insights at ~72h and ~7d.

## Sample size / confidence

Do **not** claim significance from n&lt;10 posts. Label confidence LOW until ≥8 pairs or clear directional pattern across 28 days.
