# GROWTH-EXP-2026-013 — Direct CTA vs soft CTA

**Status:** READY  
**Jobs:** AUDIT_CONVERSION / TRAFFIC

## Hypothesis

Soft CTAs (“See the free audit checklist we use”) produce more qualified clicks than hard CTAs (“Book now / Buy now”) for cold Facebook audiences, while hard CTAs may win for warm followers.

## Primary metric

Facebook-attributed audit starts

## Secondary

Link clicks, engagements, bounce/quality signals in GA4 (operator review)

## Constraint

No engagement bait CTAs.
