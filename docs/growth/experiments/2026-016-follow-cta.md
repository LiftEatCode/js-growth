# GROWTH-EXP-2026-016 — Follow CTA vs no follow CTA

**Status:** READY  
**Job:** FOLLOWER_GROWTH

## Hypothesis

A soft, non-bait follow invitation on high-reach posts increases net follows vs identical posts without a follow CTA.

## Primary metric

Net follower change (Page) in the 7 days after post (directional only at low volume)

## Secondary

Page visits, engagement rate

## Constraint

No “like/comment/share to…”. Soft: “Follow JS Solutions for weekly local SEO notes.”
