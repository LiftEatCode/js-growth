# Website Development Example: Medical Practice

> **Part of the JS Solutions Website Development Playbook**

---

# Purpose

This example demonstrates how JS Solutions approaches website development for medical practices and healthcare providers.

Medical websites require a unique balance of professionalism, trust, accessibility, and patient education while supporting appointment requests and long-term business growth.

This example serves as a reference implementation for:

- Primary Care
- Family Medicine
- Dental Practices
- Orthodontists
- Physical Therapy Clinics
- Chiropractic Offices
- Specialists
- Urgent Care
- Behavioral Health Practices

# Discovery Summary

Discovery typically reveals patients want answers to:

- What services do you provide?
- Who are your providers?
- Do you accept my insurance?
- How do I schedule an appointment?
- Where are you located?
- Can I trust your practice?

The website should answer these questions quickly and clearly.

# Design Strategy

Design priorities:

- Clean
- Professional
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Accessible
- Mobile-first

Use authentic photography whenever possible.

Avoid clutter and unnecessary distractions.

---

# User Experience

Visitors should immediately understand:

1. What care is provided.
2. Who provides it.
3. How to become a patient.
4. How to schedule an appointment.

Primary CTAs include:

- Request an Appointment
- Call Now
- Meet Our Providers
- Contact Us

# Conversion Strategy

Every page should encourage the next step.

Primary CTAs:

- Request an Appointment
- Call the Office
- Meet the Providers
- Contact Us

Trust elements include:

- Provider biographies
- Credentials
- Certifications
- Patient testimonials (where appropriate)
- Office photos
- Accepted insurance plans
- Years of experience

---

# Accessibility Standards

Medical websites should exceed normal accessibility expectations.

Verify:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen reader compatibility
- High contrast
- Large touch targets
- Readable typography
- Accessible forms

Accessibility improves the experience for all patients.

---

# Privacy & Compliance Considerations

Medical websites should be designed with privacy in mind.

Examples include:

- Secure forms
- HTTPS
- Spam protection
- Minimal collection of sensitive information through public forms
- Clear privacy policy

If regulated health information will be collected or transmitted, additional compliance requirements must be evaluated before implementation.

---

# Performance Standards

Target metrics:

- Lighthouse Performance: 90+
- Accessibility: 95+
- Best Practices: 95+
- SEO: 95+

Fast websites improve both user experience and search visibility.
