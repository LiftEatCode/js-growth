# Website Development Example: Local Business

> **Part of the JS Solutions Website Development Playbook**

---

# Purpose

This example demonstrates how JS Solutions approaches website development for local businesses that depend on community visibility, reputation, and customer engagement.

Unlike businesses that sell nationwide, local businesses compete primarily within their service area. Their website should help potential customers quickly understand what the business offers, why it is trustworthy, and how to get in touch.

This example serves as a reference implementation for:

- Restaurants
- Retail Stores
- Salons & Spas
- Coffee Shops
- Fitness Studios
- Attorneys
- Insurance Agencies
- Accounting Firms
- Real Estate Offices
- Pet Services
- Local Service Businesses

# Discovery Summary

Discovery usually identifies a few key customer questions:

- What does this business offer?
- Where are they located?
- What are their hours?
- Can I trust them?
- How do I contact them?
- Why should I choose them instead of a competitor?

The homepage should answer these questions within seconds.

# Design Strategy

Design priorities:

- Professional
- Clean
- Modern
- Mobile-first
- Fast
- Easy to navigate
- Brand consistent

Use authentic business photography whenever possible.

Avoid generic stock imagery if real photos are available.
