# Discovery Summary

Discovery typically reveals that homeowners want answers to a few simple questions:

- What services do you provide?
- Do you work in my area?
- Can I trust your company?
- How quickly can I get an estimate?
- Why should I choose you?

The website should answer these questions within the first few seconds.

Build a website that:

# Website Structure

Recommended pages:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Individual Service Pages
- Service Area Pages
- Projects / Gallery
- Testimonials
- Financing (if applicable)
- Blog
- Contact

---

# Design Strategy

Design priorities:

- Clean
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Mobile-first
- Easy navigation
- Strong branding
- Large calls-to-action

Use real project photography whenever possible.

Avoid stock images when authentic images are available.

---

# User Experience

Visitors should immediately understand:

1. What services are offered.
2. Where the business operates.
3. Why the company is trustworthy.
4. How to request service.

Primary calls to action include:

- Request a Free Estimate
- Schedule Service
- Call Now
- Contact Us

Phone numbers should remain visible throughout the website.

- Gutter Installation Magnolia TX

- Electrician Magnolia TX

Supporting strategy:

- Google Business Profile optimization
- Local citations
- Internal linking
- Educational blog content
- Customer reviews

# Content Strategy
