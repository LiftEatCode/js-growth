# Website Development Phase 1: Discovery

> "A successful website begins long before the first line of code."

---

# Purpose

The Discovery phase establishes a complete understanding of the client's business, goals, customers, current technology, and success criteria.

No project should move into planning until Discovery is complete.

Skipping Discovery almost always leads to unnecessary revisions, missed expectations, and poor project outcomes.

---

# Objectives

By the end of Discovery, JS Solutions should understand:

- The client's business
- Their customers
- Their services
- Their competitive advantages
- Their current website
- Their business goals
- Their marketing efforts
- Their technical environment
- Their desired outcomes

---

# Key Principle

The better we understand the business, the better technology we can build.

It is the foundation of every successful project.
