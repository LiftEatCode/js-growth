# Website Development Frequently Asked Questions

> **Part of the JS Solutions Website Development Playbook**

---

# Purpose

This document answers the most common questions encountered during the Website Development process.

It is intended as an internal reference for consultants, developers, project managers, and future AI assistants to ensure consistent communication with clients.

Whenever possible, answers should educate the client rather than simply respond to a question.

---

# General Questions

## How long does a typical website project take?

Every project is different.

Timeline depends on:

- Project scope
- Number of pages
- Custom functionality
- Content readiness
- Client response times
- Third-party integrations

Typical projects range from several weeks to several months.

Never promise an exact completion date until the project has been planned.

---

## How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on project requirements.

Factors include:

- Number of pages
- Design complexity
- Custom functionality
- Integrations
- SEO requirements
- Content creation
- Ongoing maintenance

Every project receives a custom proposal based on business goals.

---

## Why don't we quote every project immediately?

Discovery comes before pricing.

Without understanding the client's business, goals, and requirements, any estimate would be inaccurate.

Proper planning protects both the client and JS Solutions.

---

# Technology Questions

## Why do we use Next.js?

Next.js provides:

- Excellent performance
- Strong SEO capabilities
- Modern development workflow
- Scalability
- Security
- Long-term maintainability

It aligns with JS Solutions' engineering standards.

---

## Why don't we use low-code website builders?

Website builders have their place, but they often limit:

- Performance
- SEO
- Flexibility
- Scalability
- Custom integrations

Our goal is to build websites that grow with the client's business.

---

## Will the website work on mobile devices?

Yes.

Every website is developed using a mobile-first approach and tested across common devices and browsers.

---

# SEO Questions

## Is SEO included?

Every website includes a strong technical SEO foundation.

Examples include:

- Optimized page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Semantic HTML
- Sitemap
- Robots.txt
- Structured headings
- Internal linking
- Image optimization

Ongoing SEO services are offered separately.

---

## Will a new website immediately rank on Google?

No.

A well-built website improves the opportunity to rank, but search rankings depend on many factors, including:

- Competition
- Content quality
- Authority
- Backlinks
- Ongoing SEO efforts
- Time

We focus on building the best possible foundation for long-term success.

---

# Content Questions

## Who provides the content?

This depends on the project.

Content may be provided by:

- The client
- JS Solutions
- A professional copywriter
- A collaborative effort

Responsibilities should be documented during planning.

---

## Can we use AI-generated content?

AI can assist with research, outlines, and first drafts.

However, all published content should be reviewed, edited, and tailored to the client's business to ensure accuracy, originality, and quality.

---

# Design Questions

## Can the client request revisions?

Yes.

Revision expectations should be defined in the proposal.

Changes outside the approved project scope should follow the documented change request process.

---

## Can the design change during development?

Major design changes after development begins can significantly affect project timelines and cost.

Whenever possible, design should be finalized and approved before implementation begins.

---

# Development Questions

## Can new features be added later?

Yes.

One advantage of modern development is the ability to continue expanding a website as the client's business grows.

Future enhancements should be planned and documented separately.

---

## Do we guarantee specific search rankings?

No.

No ethical agency can guarantee search rankings.

Instead, we focus on following proven best practices, building technically sound websites, and supporting long-term SEO success.

---

# Launch Questions

## What happens on launch day?

Typical launch activities include:

- Production deployment
- DNS verification
- SSL verification
- Form testing
- Analytics verification
- SEO verification
- Performance review
- Client notification

Launch follows a documented checklist to reduce risk.

---

## Will there be downtime?

The goal is to minimize or eliminate downtime whenever possible.

Some infrastructure changes, such as DNS updates, may require propagation time.

---

# Maintenance Questions

## Do clients need ongoing maintenance?

## What happens after launch?

After launch, JS Solutions may provide:

- Security updates

- SEO improvements

The relationship continues beyond launch.

- Educate rather than sell.
