# Local SEO Timeline

> This document outlines the typical lifecycle of a Local SEO engagement. While every business is unique, the timeline below provides clients with realistic expectations for implementation, optimization, and measurable results.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this timeline is to communicate the phases of a Local SEO campaign, establish realistic expectations, and provide a roadmap for both the client and the JS Solutions team.

Local SEO is an ongoing process, not a one-time project. Sustainable growth takes consistency, data, and continuous improvement.

---

# Timeline Philosophy

Local SEO is a long-term investment.

While some improvements may be noticeable within the first month, meaningful and sustainable growth typically occurs over several months of consistent optimization.

Our goal is not quick wins alone, but long-term business growth.

---

# Ongoing Monthly Activities

Every month we typically:

- Review keyword rankings
- Analyze website traffic
- Monitor conversions
- Update Google Business Profile
- Review citations
- Identify technical issues
- Publish or recommend content
- Monitor competitors
- Prepare monthly reports
- Adjust strategy as needed

---

# Client Responsibilities

Clients contribute to success by:

- Providing timely feedback
- Approving requested content
- Responding to review requests
- Keeping business information current
- Communicating business changes
- Participating in strategy meetings

Local SEO works best as a partnership.

---

# Factors That Affect Timeline

Results may vary depending on:

- Market competition
- Website quality
- Domain authority
- Google Business Profile condition
- Existing content
- Technical issues
- Review profile
- Industry trends

No two businesses will follow the exact same timeline.

---

# Setting Expectations

Clients should understand:

- SEO is not instant.
- Rankings naturally fluctuate.
- Business growth is measured over time.
- Consistency produces long-term results.

Our focus is on sustainable growth rather than temporary gains.
