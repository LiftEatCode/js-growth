# Local SEO Keyword Research

> Keyword research is the foundation of every successful Local SEO campaign. The goal is to identify how potential customers search for a client's products and services, prioritize opportunities, and build a long-term SEO strategy around those searches.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of keyword research is to identify the search terms that are most likely to generate qualified traffic, leads, and customers.

Our objective is not to rank for the highest-volume keywords.

Our objective is to rank for the keywords that generate business.

---

# Objectives

Every keyword research project should:

- Understand customer search behavior
- Identify commercial intent keywords
- Discover local search opportunities
- Prioritize high-value services
- Support website architecture
- Guide content strategy
- Improve Google Business Profile visibility
- Build long-term ranking opportunities

---

# Keyword Research Philosophy

At JS Solutions we prioritize:

Business Value

over

Search Volume

A keyword with 50 highly qualified searches per month is often more valuable than one with 5,000 informational searches.

Every keyword should answer one question:

> Will ranking for this keyword help grow the client's business?

---

# Keyword Categories

Every keyword should be categorized.

## Primary Service Keywords

Examples:

- Auto Repair
- HVAC Repair
- Emergency Plumber
- Personal Injury Lawyer

These represent the client's core services.

---

## Location Keywords

Examples:

- Magnolia TX
- Spring TX
- Tomball TX
- The Woodlands TX

Every primary service should eventually be mapped to target locations.

---

## Service + Location

Examples:

- Auto Repair Magnolia TX
- AC Repair Spring TX
- Dentist Tomball TX

These are often the highest-priority Local SEO keywords.

---

## Long-Tail Keywords

Examples:

- Brake Repair Near Me
- Cost of AC Repair
- Best Oil Change Magnolia TX

These typically have lower competition and higher conversion rates.

---

## Informational Keywords

Examples:

- Why is my AC blowing warm air?
- How often should I change my oil?
- Signs of a bad alternator

These support blog content and topical authority.

---

## Branded Keywords

Examples:

- JS Solutions
- Tha Shop
- Business name

Monitor branded searches separately.

# Research Process

## Step 1

Understand the Business

Review:

- Services
- Customers
- Service Area
- Revenue Drivers
- Business Goals

---

## Step 2

Identify Core Services

List every service the business provides.

Rank each service by:

- Revenue
- Profitability
- Business Priority

---

## Step 3

Generate Seed Keywords

Create an initial keyword list based on:

- Services
- Customer language
- Existing website
- Google Business Profile
- Competitors

---

## Step 4

Expand Keywords

Use SEO tools to identify:

- Variations
- Questions
- Related searches
- Long-tail opportunities
- Location modifiers

---

## Step 5

Evaluate Keywords

Review:

- Search Volume
- Keyword Difficulty
- Commercial Intent
- Local Intent
- Competition
- Business Value

---

## Step 6

Prioritize

Every keyword should receive a priority.

### High Priority

High business value

High commercial intent

Strong local relevance

---

### Medium Priority

Good opportunity

Moderate competition

Supports primary services

---

### Low Priority

Informational

Future content

Low business impact

---

# Keyword Mapping

Every primary keyword should eventually map to:

- Website Page
- Service
- City
- Google Business Profile
- Blog Content
- Internal Links

No important keyword should exist without a destination.

---

# Competitor Validation

Review:

- Competitor rankings
- Competitor service pages
- Competitor content
- Google Business Profile categories
- Frequently used terms

Do not copy competitors.

Use competitor research to identify opportunities.

---

# Deliverables

Every keyword research engagement should produce:

- Master Keyword List
- Primary Keyword List
- Secondary Keyword List
- Service Keyword Map
- Location Keyword Map
- Content Opportunities
- Priority Recommendations

---

# Success Criteria

Keyword research is complete when:

- Core services are represented
- Primary locations are represented
- High-value keywords are identified
- Content opportunities are documented
- Keywords are prioritized
- Strategy is approved

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Chasing search volume alone
- Ignoring local intent
- Targeting too many keywords on one page
- Ignoring commercial intent
- Copying competitor strategies
- Focusing only on rankings instead of business outcomes
