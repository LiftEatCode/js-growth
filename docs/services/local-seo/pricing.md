# Local SEO Pricing

> This document outlines the pricing philosophy, engagement models, service packages, and proposal guidelines for the JS Solutions Local SEO service. It is intended for internal use when preparing proposals and recommending solutions for prospective clients.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish a consistent pricing methodology for Local SEO services.

Pricing should reflect the value delivered to the client, the complexity of the engagement, and the resources required—not simply the number of hours worked.

Our objective is to build long-term client relationships through transparent, fair, and sustainable pricing.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

JS Solutions follows a value-based pricing model.

We price our services based on:

- Business impact
- Scope of work
- Competition
- Market size
- Client goals
- Ongoing maintenance requirements

Our goal is to create pricing that is profitable for JS Solutions while delivering measurable value for the client.

---

# Pricing Principles

Every proposal should be:

- Transparent
- Consistent
- Easy to understand
- Based on business objectives
- Flexible when appropriate

Avoid overwhelming clients with unnecessary complexity.

---

# Factors That Influence Pricing

Every proposal should consider:

## Website Size

Examples:

- Number of pages
- Service pages
- Location pages
- Blog content

---

## Business Size

Examples:

- Single location
- Multiple locations
- Franchise
- Service-area business

---

## Competition

Evaluate:

- Local competition
- Market saturation
- Competitor authority
- Required effort

More competitive markets typically require greater investment.

---

## Current Website Condition

Review:

- Technical SEO
- Website quality
- Existing content
- Conversion optimization
- Performance

Websites requiring extensive improvements may require a Website Development project before or alongside Local SEO.

---

## Google Business Profile

Determine:

- Profile ownership
- Optimization status
- Review quality
- Category accuracy
- Existing visibility

---

## Content Requirements

Consider:

- Service pages
- Location pages
- Blog content
- FAQs
- Landing pages

Content creation significantly affects project scope.

---

## Ongoing Support

Determine the expected level of monthly support.

Examples:

- Reporting
- Technical monitoring
- Content updates
- Citation management
- Google Business Profile management

# Proposal Guidelines

Every proposal should include:

- Business goals
- Scope of work
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Investment
- Success metrics
- Assumptions
- Exclusions

Clients should clearly understand what is included before work begins.

---

# Scope Changes

If project requirements change after approval:

- Document the requested change.
- Evaluate the impact on scope, timeline, and cost.
- Present a revised proposal or change order for approval before beginning additional work.

Never assume work outside the agreed scope is included.

---

# Payment Structure

Typical engagement models include:

- One-time project fee
- Monthly recurring service
- Initial setup plus recurring maintenance

Payment schedules should be clearly defined in the proposal.

---

# Pricing Review

Pricing should be reviewed periodically based on:

- Market conditions
- Service demand
- Internal costs
- Client results
- Business growth

Avoid changing pricing frequently for existing clients without clear communication.

---

# Common Pricing Mistakes

Avoid:

- Charging only by the hour
- Competing solely on price
- Underestimating project complexity
- Including unlimited revisions
- Failing to define scope
- Discounting without a strategic reason

Remember:

The lowest-priced provider rarely delivers the highest value.

---

# Success Criteria

A pricing proposal is successful when:

- The scope is clearly defined.
- Deliverables are understood.
- Expectations are aligned.
- The engagement is profitable.
- The client understands the value being delivered.
