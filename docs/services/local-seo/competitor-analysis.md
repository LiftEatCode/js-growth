# Local SEO Competitor Analysis

> Competitor analysis helps identify opportunities, strengths, weaknesses, and market trends by evaluating the online presence of businesses competing for the same customers and search visibility.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of competitor analysis is to understand the competitive landscape before making optimization recommendations.

Rather than copying competitors, JS Solutions uses competitor research to identify opportunities for differentiation and growth.

---

# Objectives

Every competitor analysis should:

- Identify primary competitors
- Understand the local search landscape
- Evaluate competitor strengths
- Identify competitor weaknesses
- Discover keyword opportunities
- Analyze Google Business Profiles
- Review website quality
- Support long-term SEO strategy

---

# Competitor Categories

## Direct Competitors

Businesses offering the same services in the same geographic market.

Examples:

- Local auto repair shops
- Nearby HVAC companies
- Local dentists

---

## Search Competitors

Businesses ranking for target keywords.

Search competitors are not always direct business competitors.

Examples:

- Large directories
- National franchises
- Industry websites
- Aggregators

---

## Aspirational Competitors

Businesses with exceptional online visibility.

These businesses provide ideas and best practices but should not be copied directly.

---

# Research Process

## Step 1

Identify Competitors

Gather:

- 3–5 direct competitors
- 3–5 search competitors
- 1–3 aspirational competitors

Document:

- Business Name
- Website
- Google Business Profile
- Primary Service Area

---

## Step 2

Analyze Websites

Review:

- Website design
- Navigation
- Mobile experience
- Page speed
- Service pages
- Location pages
- Blog content
- Calls to action
- Contact methods

Document strengths and weaknesses.

---

## Step 3

Analyze Google Business Profiles

Review:

- Categories
- Reviews
- Review rating
- Review count
- Photos
- Business description
- Services
- Products
- Questions & Answers
- Posts
- Business hours

---

## Step 4

Analyze Keywords

Identify:

- Primary keywords
- Secondary keywords
- Service keywords
- Location keywords
- Long-tail keywords

Determine:

- Ranking opportunities
- Content gaps
- Missed opportunities

---

## Step 5

Analyze Content

Review:

- Service pages
- Blog articles
- FAQs
- Resources
- Internal linking
- Location pages

Identify:

- Missing topics
- Strong content
- Weak content
- Opportunities

---

## Step 6

Analyze Backlinks

Review:

- Referring domains
- Local citations
- Community involvement
- Sponsorships
- Local partnerships

Focus on legitimate local authority rather than backlink quantity.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Score each competitor in the following areas.

## Website

- Design
- Speed
- Mobile Experience
- Accessibility
- Calls to Action

---

## SEO

- Metadata
- Content Quality
- Internal Linking
- Technical SEO
- Keyword Targeting

---

## Google Business Profile

- Completeness
- Reviews
- Photos
- Categories
- Activity

---

## Local Presence

- Citations
- Community involvement
- Local partnerships
- Brand recognition

---

## Overall

Assign an overall competitive score based on findings.

---

# Opportunity Analysis

Every competitor analysis should identify:

## Quick Wins

Improvements that can be implemented immediately.

Examples:

- Missing metadata
- Poor GBP optimization
- Weak service pages

---

## Medium-Term Opportunities

Projects requiring moderate effort.

Examples:

- New location pages
- Blog content
- Review strategy
- Citation cleanup

---

## Long-Term Opportunities

Large strategic initiatives.

Examples:

- Content authority
- Backlink acquisition
- Community partnerships
- Brand development

---

# Deliverables

Each competitor analysis should produce:

- Competitor Summary
- Website Comparison
- Google Business Profile Comparison
- Keyword Gap Analysis
- Content Gap Analysis
- Opportunity List
- Strategic Recommendations

---

# Success Criteria

Competitor analysis is complete when:

- Competitors have been identified
- Websites reviewed
- Google Business Profiles reviewed
- Keywords analyzed
- Content analyzed
- Opportunities documented
- Recommendations prepared

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Copying competitor content
- Chasing every competitor keyword
- Ignoring business goals
- Focusing only on rankings
- Comparing to irrelevant competitors
- Overlooking local businesses with strong offline reputations
