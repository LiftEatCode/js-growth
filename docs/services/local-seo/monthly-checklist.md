# Local SEO Monthly Checklist

> This checklist defines the recurring monthly activities required to maintain, improve, and report on every Local SEO campaign. Completing these tasks consistently ensures continuous optimization, measurable progress, and long-term business growth.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this checklist is to standardize monthly Local SEO activities across every client engagement.

Every campaign should receive consistent attention regardless of industry, business size, or service package.

SEO is an ongoing process of monitoring, optimization, testing, and continuous improvement.

---

# Monthly Workflow

```text
Review Performance
        ↓
Identify Opportunities
        ↓
Implement Improvements
        ↓
Verify Results
        ↓
Prepare Report
        ↓
Meet with Client
        ↓
Repeat
```

# Definition of Done

The monthly cycle is complete when:

- Website performance has been reviewed.
- Rankings have been evaluated.
- Google Business Profile has been optimized.
- Technical issues have been addressed.
- Content opportunities have been identified.
- Reports have been delivered.
- Recommendations have been documented.
- Next month's priorities have been established.
