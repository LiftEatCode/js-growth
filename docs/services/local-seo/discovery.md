# Local SEO Discovery

> The discovery phase establishes the foundation for every successful Local SEO engagement. During discovery, we gather information about the client's business, goals, customers, competitors, website, and current online presence.

---

# Purpose

The goal of the discovery phase is to fully understand the client's business before making SEO recommendations.

Successful SEO is built on understanding the business—not just the website.

Discovery helps ensure recommendations are aligned with the client's objectives, target audience, service area, and competitive landscape.

---

# Objectives

By the end of discovery, we should understand:

- The client's business
- Their products or services
- Their target customers
- Their geographic service area
- Their competitors
- Their current marketing efforts
- Their website
- Their Google Business Profile
- Their goals
- Their success metrics

---

# Discovery Checklist

## Business Information

Collect the following:

- Business Name
- Website
- Phone Number
- Email Address
- Physical Address
- Service Area
- Years in Business
- Number of Employees
- Business Hours

---

## Business Overview

Understand:

- What does the business do?
- What services generate the most revenue?
- Which services are most profitable?
- What makes the business different?
- What problems do they solve?
- Why do customers choose them?

---

## Target Audience

Identify:

- Ideal customer
- Customer demographics
- Customer pain points
- Buying behavior
- Primary service locations
- Secondary service locations

---

## Services

Document:

- Primary services
- Secondary services
- Seasonal services
- High-value services
- Services to prioritize

---

## Service Area

Identify:

- Primary city
- Surrounding cities
- Counties served
- Travel radius
- Physical storefront or service business

---

## Current Marketing

Ask:

- Do you currently perform SEO?
- Do you run Google Ads?
- Do you advertise on Facebook?
- Are you active on social media?
- Do you publish blog content?
- Do you send email newsletters?
- Who currently manages marketing?

---

## Website Review

Determine:

- Website platform
- Website age
- Mobile friendliness
- Website speed
- Existing SEO
- Existing content
- Contact forms
- Conversion opportunities

---

## Google Business Profile

Review:

- Ownership status
- Verification status
- Categories
- Service Areas
- Photos
- Reviews
- Q&A
- Business Description
- Products
- Services

---

## Competitors

Identify:

At minimum collect:

- 3 local competitors
- 3 online competitors

Ask:

Who do customers compare you to?

Who ranks above you?

Who do you admire?

---

## Current Performance

If available collect:

- Organic Traffic
- Phone Calls
- Leads
- Conversions
- Google Search Console
- Google Analytics
- GBP Insights

---

## Goals

Ask:

What would success look like six months from now?

Examples:

- More phone calls
- More appointments
- More estimate requests
- Better rankings
- More website traffic
- More reviews
- Increased revenue

---

# Discovery Questions

## Business

- Tell me about your business.
- What makes your business unique?
- What services are most important?
- What are your biggest challenges?

---

## Customers

- Who is your ideal customer?
- Where are your customers located?
- What problems do they need solved?

---

## Competition

- Who are your biggest competitors?
- Who would you like to outperform?
- What do your competitors do well?

---

## Marketing

- What has worked previously?
- What hasn't worked?
- What marketing are you currently investing in?

---

## Website

- Are you happy with your current website?
- What would you improve?
- Does it generate leads today?

---

## SEO

- Have you hired an SEO company before?
- What were the results?
- What are your biggest frustrations?

---

## Success

Ask the client:

"If we were meeting one year from today, what would need to happen for you to consider this project a success?"

This answer should guide the entire engagement.

---

# Discovery Deliverables

The discovery phase should produce:

- Client Notes
- Business Summary
- Target Audience Summary
- Competitor List
- Service Area Map
- Initial Website Audit
- Initial Google Business Profile Review
- Recommended Strategy
- Project Scope

---

# Exit Criteria

Discovery is complete when:

- Business goals are understood
- Services are documented
- Service area is defined
- Competitors are identified
- Website has been reviewed
- Google Business Profile has been reviewed
- Client expectations are documented
- Success metrics are agreed upon

Only after these items are complete should the project move into Keyword Research.
