# Local SEO Client Qualification

> Before beginning any Local SEO engagement, JS Solutions performs a qualification process to determine whether the client is a good fit for our services, goals, and long-term partnership approach.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of client qualification is to determine whether a prospective client is likely to achieve success through Local SEO and whether their expectations, budget, and commitment align with the services provided by JS Solutions.

Not every business is an ideal fit for Local SEO, and not every prospect is an ideal client.

A successful engagement requires mutual commitment and realistic expectations.

---

# Objectives

The qualification process helps determine:

- Whether Local SEO is the right solution
- Whether the client's goals are achievable
- Whether the business is committed to long-term growth
- Whether expectations are realistic
- Whether JS Solutions can provide measurable value

---

# Ideal Client Profile

The best Local SEO clients typically:

- Operate within a defined geographic area
- Have an established business
- Want long-term growth rather than quick fixes
- Value data-driven decision making
- Are willing to invest in their online presence
- Understand that SEO is an ongoing process

---

# Qualification Criteria

## Business

Confirm:

- Business is currently operating
- Business information is accurate
- Services are clearly defined
- Service area is established
- Website exists (or a new website project is planned)

---

## Website

Evaluate:

- Website quality
- Mobile responsiveness
- Website performance
- Existing SEO
- Conversion opportunities

A poor website does not disqualify a client, but it may require a Website Development project before SEO efforts begin.

---

## Google Business Profile

Determine:

- Profile exists
- Ownership can be verified
- Business information is accurate
- Categories are appropriate
- Reviews are actively managed

---

## Service Area

Confirm:

- Primary city
- Secondary cities
- Travel radius
- Physical location or service-area business

---

## Marketing Commitment

Discuss:

- Current marketing efforts
- Willingness to create content
- Ability to respond to reviews
- Availability for monthly strategy meetings
- Long-term commitment

---

## Budget

Determine whether the client's budget aligns with the scope of work required.

Consider:

- Initial optimization
- Monthly SEO
- Website improvements
- Content creation
- Additional marketing services

If expectations exceed the available budget, discuss alternative approaches or phased implementation.

---

## Expectations

Ensure the client understands:

- SEO is a long-term investment
- Rankings cannot be guaranteed
- Results vary by competition
- Success depends on multiple factors
- Consistency is critical

---

# Red Flags

Proceed carefully if the client:

- Wants immediate first-page rankings
- Expects guaranteed results
- Has unrealistic budget expectations
- Refuses technical recommendations
- Is unwilling to make website improvements
- Is unwilling to verify or manage their Google Business Profile
- Frequently changes business priorities
- Has a history of switching SEO providers every few months

One red flag does not automatically disqualify a client, but multiple red flags should prompt additional discussion before moving forward.

---

# Qualification Questions

## Business

- How long have you been in business?
- What services are most important?
- What areas do you serve?
- What makes your business different?

---

## Goals

- What are your primary business goals?
- What does success look like?
- Which services would you like to grow?
- What markets would you like to expand into?

---

## Marketing

- Have you invested in SEO before?
- What worked well?
- What didn't work?
- What marketing channels currently generate customers?

---

## Website

- Are you satisfied with your current website?
- Does it generate leads?
- Are you willing to make improvements if necessary?

---

## Expectations

- What timeline are you expecting?
- What concerns do you have about SEO?
- What would make this engagement successful?

---

# Qualification Outcomes

## Qualified

The client is an excellent fit.

Proceed to:

- Strategy
- Proposal
- Project kickoff

---

## Qualified with Recommendations

The client is a good fit but should address additional items such as:

- Website redesign
- Google Business Profile improvements
- Content creation
- Technical issues

These items should be included in the proposal.

---

## Not Currently Qualified

The client is not currently a good fit.

Reasons may include:

- Unrealistic expectations
- Insufficient budget
- Inactive business
- Poor communication
- Services outside JS Solutions' expertise

Provide helpful recommendations where appropriate.

---

# Exit Criteria

A client is considered qualified when:

- Business goals are understood
- Expectations are realistic
- Budget aligns with project scope
- Service area is defined
- Website has been evaluated
- Google Business Profile has been reviewed
- Long-term commitment has been discussed
- Both parties agree on the direction of the project
