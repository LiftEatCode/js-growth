# Citation Management

> Citation management ensures a business's information is accurate, consistent, and trustworthy across the internet. Consistent citations strengthen local search visibility, improve customer trust, and support Google Business Profile rankings.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of citation management is to maintain consistent business information across online directories, local listings, industry websites, and data providers.

Search engines use citations to validate the legitimacy and trustworthiness of a business.

Consistent citations improve Local SEO while inconsistent information creates confusion for both customers and search engines.

---

# Objectives

Every citation management project should:

- Improve NAP consistency
- Increase local trust
- Strengthen Local SEO
- Improve business discoverability
- Eliminate duplicate listings
- Correct outdated information
- Expand local directory coverage

---

# Citation Philosophy

Citation management is about consistency—not quantity.

A smaller number of accurate, authoritative citations is significantly more valuable than hundreds of inconsistent listings.

Accuracy always takes priority over volume.

# Business Information

Before creating or updating citations, verify the official business information.

Collect:

- Business Name
- Address
- Phone Number
- Website
- Email
- Business Hours
- Service Area
- Categories
- Business Description

This information becomes the authoritative source for all citations.

---

# NAP Consistency

The following information must remain consistent across every listing.

## Business Name

Use the official legal or public-facing business name.

Avoid unnecessary keyword stuffing.

---

## Address

Use the same formatting everywhere.

Example:

```
123 Main Street
Magnolia, TX 77354
```

---

## Phone Number

Use one primary local phone number whenever possible.

Avoid switching between multiple phone numbers.

---

## Website

Use the preferred canonical website URL consistently.

Example:

```
https://www.example.com
```

---

# Citation Audit

Review existing listings for:

- Incorrect addresses
- Old phone numbers
- Duplicate listings
- Broken website links
- Missing categories
- Missing hours
- Missing descriptions
- Incorrect business names

Document every issue before making updates.

---

# Duplicate Listings

Identify duplicate business listings.

When possible:

- Merge duplicates
- Remove outdated listings
- Claim ownership
- Redirect users to the primary listing

Duplicate listings dilute local authority.

---

# Directory Prioritization

Prioritize updates in the following order.

## Tier 1

Highest Priority

- Google Business Profile
- Apple Maps
- Bing Places

---

## Tier 2

Major Directories

- Yelp
- Facebook
- Better Business Bureau
- Yellow Pages

---

## Tier 3

Industry Directories

Examples:

- Automotive
- Medical
- Legal
- Home Services

Prioritize directories relevant to the client's industry.

---

## Tier 4

Local Directories

Examples:

- Chamber of Commerce
- Local business associations
- Community directories

These often provide valuable local authority.

---

# Citation Creation

When creating new listings:

Verify:

- NAP consistency
- Categories
- Website URL
- Business Description
- Photos
- Business Hours

Every listing should be complete whenever possible.

---

# Business Descriptions

Descriptions should:

- Clearly explain services
- Mention primary service areas naturally
- Highlight business strengths
- Remain consistent with the website

Avoid copying descriptions word-for-word across every directory.

---

# Categories

Select the most accurate category available.

Do not choose unrelated categories simply to increase visibility.

---

# Images

Where supported, upload:

- Logo
- Exterior photos
- Interior photos
- Team photos
- Service photos

Images improve both trust and engagement.

---

# Ongoing Maintenance

Review citations regularly for:

- Business changes
- New directories
- Duplicate listings
- Broken links
- Outdated information

Citation management is an ongoing process.

---

# Quality Assurance Checklist

Verify:

- Business Name consistent
- Address consistent
- Phone Number consistent
- Website correct
- Categories correct
- Hours updated
- Photos uploaded
- Description completed
- Duplicate listings removed
