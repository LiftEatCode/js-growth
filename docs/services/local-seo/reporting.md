# Local SEO Reporting

> Reporting provides clients with a clear understanding of campaign performance, completed work, measurable results, and future opportunities. Reports should communicate business impact rather than simply presenting SEO metrics.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of reporting is to demonstrate progress, communicate value, identify opportunities, and guide future optimization efforts.

Reports should answer one simple question:

**"Is the client's business growing because of our work?"**

SEO reporting is not about overwhelming clients with data.

It is about providing meaningful insights that support business decisions.

---

# Objectives

Every report should:

- Demonstrate progress
- Highlight completed work
- Measure business outcomes
- Identify opportunities
- Recommend next steps
- Build client confidence

---

# Reporting Philosophy

At JS Solutions we report on business growth—not vanity metrics.

Rankings matter.

Traffic matters.

But the most important metrics are:

- Phone Calls
- Leads
- Appointments
- Revenue
- Return on Investment

Everything else supports those outcomes.

# Reporting Schedule

## Monthly

Every client receives a monthly performance report.

---

## Quarterly

A strategic review should be completed every quarter.

- Strategy adjustments

Review long-term growth.

Discuss:

---

# Monthly Report Structure

Every report should include the following sections.

---

## Executive Summary

Provide a concise overview.

Examples:

- Overall performance
- Major improvements
- Challenges
- Recommendations

The executive summary should be understandable by any business owner regardless of technical knowledge.

---

## Work Completed

Document all completed work.

Examples:

- Technical SEO improvements
- Metadata optimization
- New content
- Google Business Profile updates
- Citation corrections
- Internal linking improvements

Clients should clearly understand what work was performed.

---

## Website Performance

Review:

- Organic Sessions
- Users
- Page Views
- Landing Pages
- Conversion Rate

Compare against previous reporting periods whenever possible.

---

## Keyword Performance

Review:

- Primary keyword rankings
- Secondary keyword rankings
- New ranking opportunities
- Significant ranking changes

Avoid focusing on minor daily fluctuations.

---

## Google Business Profile Performance

Review:

- Search Views
- Maps Views
- Website Clicks
- Phone Calls
- Direction Requests
- Messages
- Photo Views

These metrics often provide the clearest picture of Local SEO performance.

---

## Conversion Metrics

Measure:

- Phone Calls
- Contact Forms
- Appointment Requests
- Quote Requests
- Bookings
- Revenue (when available)

Conversions are the primary measure of campaign success.

---

## Technical Health

Review:

- Core Web Vitals
- Website Speed
- Crawl Errors
- Indexing Issues
- Broken Links
- Security

Highlight any issues requiring attention.

---

## Review Performance

Track:

- New Reviews
- Average Rating
- Review Responses
- Reputation Trends

Reviews contribute to both customer trust and Local SEO.

---

## Competitor Summary

Provide updates on:

- Competitor rankings
- New competitors
- Market changes
- New opportunities

Focus on meaningful competitive insights rather than exhaustive comparisons.

---

## Opportunities

Identify:

- New content opportunities
- New keyword opportunities
- Website improvements
- Google Business Profile enhancements
- Citation opportunities
- Technical improvements

Every report should include actionable recommendations.

---

## Next Month's Plan

Outline priorities.

Examples:

- New service pages
- Technical fixes
- Content creation
- Citation updates
- Google Business Profile optimization

Clients should understand exactly what comes next.

---

---

# Report Quality Standards

Every report should be:

- Accurate
- Easy to understand
- Actionable
- Honest
- Transparent
- Focused on business value

Avoid excessive technical jargon.

---

# Common Reporting Mistakes

Avoid:

- Reporting rankings without context
- Focusing only on traffic
- Ignoring conversions
- Overwhelming clients with data
- Hiding negative trends
- Making guarantees

Clients value honesty more than perfection.
