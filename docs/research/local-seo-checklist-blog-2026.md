## Claim 1 — Local ranking factors

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **SOURCE** | Google Business Profile Help — Tips to improve your local ranking on Google |
| **URL** | https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091 |
| **ACCESS DATE** | 2026-08-25 |
| **FACT** | Local results are mainly based on **relevance**, **distance**, and **prominence** (also described as popularity). There is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. |
| **ARTICLE IMPLICATION** | Checklist should prioritize complete/accurate business info (relevance), not “hacks.” Distance is largely not controllable. Reviews and web presence relate to prominence — state carefully. |
| **PROVENANCE** | OFFICIAL_GOOGLE |

## Claim 2 — Complete / accurate Business Profile info

| Field | Value |

## Claim 3 — Reviews and prominence

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **SOURCE** | Same GBP Help article |
| **URL** | https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091 |
| **ACCESS DATE** | 2026-08-25 |
| **FACT** | Prominence reflects how well-known a business is; Google notes this is also based on signals like website links and reviews. More reviews and positive ratings **can help** local ranking. Responding to reviews shows customers their feedback is valued. |
| **ARTICLE IMPLICATION** | Say reviews **can help prominence**, not “reviews are the #1 ranking factor” or guaranteed lift. Encourage legitimate review practices and replies. |
| **PROVENANCE** | OFFICIAL_GOOGLE |

## Claim 4 — Fake engagement / review restrictions

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **SOURCE** | Google Business Profile Help — Business Profile restrictions for policy violations |
| **URL** | https://support.google.com/business/answer/14114287 |
| **ACCESS DATE** | 2026-08-25 |
| **FACT** | Fake and/or incentivized reviews are taken seriously; violative activity can lead to review restrictions (cannot receive new reviews, unpublished ratings, consumer warnings). |
| **ARTICLE IMPLICATION** | Warn against fake/incentivized reviews. “No review gating” is industry practice aligned with Google’s fake-engagement posture; phrase as policy-safe: do not selectively solicit only positive reviews / discourage negatives. |
| **PROVENANCE** | OFFICIAL_GOOGLE (+ INFERENCE for “gating” wording where Maps UGC policy language is paraphrased carefully) |

## Claim 6 — Structured data purpose

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **SOURCE** | Google Search Central — General Structured Data Guidelines |
| **URL** | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies |
| **ACCESS DATE** | 2026-08-25 |
| **FACT** | Structured data must match visible content; must not mislead; eligibility for rich results ≠ guaranteed display. Violations can lead to spam treatment / manual actions. |
| **ARTICLE IMPLICATION** | Structured data helps Google **understand** entities; do not claim “schema improves rankings.” No FAQ schema for SEO chasing. No fake review schema. |
| **PROVENANCE** | OFFICIAL_GOOGLE |
