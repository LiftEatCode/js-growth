# Standard Operating Procedure: Project Kickoff

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the process for officially starting a client project after the proposal has been approved and Discovery has been completed.

The objective is to align all stakeholders, confirm expectations, finalize project logistics, and ensure the team is prepared to begin execution.

A successful kickoff meeting creates momentum, reduces uncertainty, and establishes a strong working relationship between JS Solutions and the client.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to every client project including:

- Website Development
- Local SEO
- AI Automation
- Analytics
- Custom Software

No project work should begin before the kickoff process has been completed.

---

# Responsibilities

## Project Manager

Responsible for:

- Scheduling the kickoff meeting
- Preparing the meeting agenda
- Reviewing project documentation
- Assigning project tasks
- Confirming timelines

---

## Technical Lead

Responsible for:

- Reviewing technical requirements
- Confirming implementation approach
- Identifying dependencies
- Answering technical questions

---

## Client

Responsible for:

- Attending the kickoff meeting
- Confirming project priorities
- Identifying key stakeholders
- Providing remaining assets
- Approving the communication plan

---

# Estimated Time

Preparation: 30–45 minutes

Kickoff Meeting: 60–90 minutes

Documentation & Follow-Up: 30 minutes

Total: Approximately 2–3 hours

---

# Prerequisites

Before the kickoff meeting:

- Proposal approved
- Discovery completed
- Scope finalized
- Timeline approved
- Required access received (or scheduled)
- Project workspace created

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Project Summary

Review:

- Business goals
- Project objectives
- Approved scope
- Timeline
- Budget
- Deliverables

Ensure all internal team members understand the project.

---

## Step 2 — Introduce the Project Team

Introduce:

- Project Manager
- Technical Lead
- Designers
- Developers
- SEO Specialists
- Other contributors

Clarify each person's role and responsibilities.

---

## Step 3 — Confirm Scope

Review:

- Included services
- Deliverables
- Milestones
- Client responsibilities
- Items outside project scope

Address any questions before work begins.

---

## Step 4 — Review Timeline

Discuss:

- Project phases
- Major milestones
- Review periods
- Target launch date
- Expected response times

Confirm the timeline remains achievable.

---

## Step 5 — Review Communication Plan

Confirm:

- Primary communication platform
- Meeting cadence
- Status update frequency
- Primary points of contact
- Escalation process

Document agreed expectations.

---

## Step 6 — Verify Required Assets

Confirm receipt of:

- Logo
- Brand guidelines
- Images
- Website copy
- Videos
- Account access
- Existing documentation

Create an action list for any missing items.

---

## Step 7 — Review Technical Requirements

Discuss:

- Hosting
- Domain management
- DNS
- Third-party integrations
- Email services
- Analytics
- Security considerations

Identify outstanding technical dependencies.

---

## Step 8 — Assign Initial Tasks

Assign responsibilities for:

- Content collection
- Design preparation
- Technical setup
- Client deliverables
- Internal deliverables

Record ownership and due dates.

---

## Step 9 — Confirm Success Metrics

Review how project success will be measured.

Examples include:

- Website launch
- Lead generation
- Increased traffic
- Search rankings
- Conversion improvements
- Performance benchmarks

Ensure everyone agrees on the desired outcomes.

---

## Step 10 — Document Action Items

Create a list of:

- Open questions
- Outstanding assets
- Assigned tasks
- Deadlines
- Risks

Distribute meeting notes within one business day.

---

# Quality Standards

Every kickoff meeting should:

- Establish confidence
- Eliminate ambiguity
- Clarify responsibilities
- Confirm timelines
- Build enthusiasm
- Prepare the team for execution

Clients should leave the meeting knowing exactly what happens next.

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Missing assets | Create a content collection plan with deadlines |
| Scope confusion | Review the approved proposal and document clarifications |
| Timeline concerns | Adjust milestones before development begins |
| Missing stakeholders | Schedule a follow-up meeting with decision makers |
| Technical dependencies | Assign ownership and target completion dates |

---

# Success Criteria

This SOP is complete when:

- Kickoff meeting held
- Project scope confirmed
- Timeline reviewed
- Communication plan established
- Team introductions completed
- Technical requirements confirmed
- Initial tasks assigned
- Meeting notes distributed

The project is now ready to enter active execution.
