# Standard Operating Procedure: Discovery Meeting

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the process for conducting a Discovery Meeting with a new client.

The objective is to understand the client's business, goals, challenges, target audience, technical requirements, and expectations before planning or development begins.

A successful discovery meeting reduces misunderstandings, improves project planning, and establishes a strong working relationship.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to every new client project, including:

- Website Development
- Local SEO
- AI Automation
- Analytics
- Custom Software

Every project begins with Discovery.

---

# Responsibilities

## Project Manager

Responsible for:

- Scheduling the meeting
- Leading the discussion
- Documenting notes
- Managing time
- Confirming next steps

---

## Technical Lead

Responsible for:

- Asking technical questions
- Identifying potential risks
- Recommending solutions
- Clarifying technical requirements

---

## Client

Responsible for:

- Explaining business goals
- Providing project requirements
- Identifying key stakeholders
- Asking questions
- Confirming expectations

---

# Prerequisites

Before the meeting:

- Client onboarding completed
- Welcome email sent
- Discovery questionnaire reviewed (if completed)
- Proposal available for reference
- Meeting agenda shared

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Prepare for the Meeting

Review:

- Proposal
- Discovery Questionnaire
- Previous communications
- Existing website
- Competitor websites

Prepare a list of discussion topics and questions.

---

## Step 2 — Introductions

Introduce all attendees.

Review:

- Roles
- Responsibilities
- Meeting objectives
- Estimated meeting duration

Create a collaborative environment from the beginning.

---

## Step 3 — Understand the Business

Discuss:

- Company history
- Products and services
- Mission
- Vision
- Competitive advantages
- Current challenges

Document key business insights.

---

## Step 4 — Define Business Goals

Ask questions such as:

- What does success look like?
- What are your biggest business goals?
- What problems are you trying to solve?
- What would make this project a success?

Record measurable objectives whenever possible.

---

## Step 5 — Identify the Target Audience

Discuss:

- Ideal customers
- Geographic service area
- Customer demographics
- Common customer questions
- Customer pain points

These insights will influence design, content, and marketing strategy.

---

## Step 6 — Review Existing Assets

Determine what already exists:

- Website
- Logo
- Brand Guidelines
- Photography
- Videos
- Marketing Materials
- Existing Content

Identify any missing assets.

---

## Step 7 — Discuss Technical Requirements

Review:

- Required functionality
- Third-party integrations
- Hosting
- Domains
- Email services
- Analytics
- Existing software

Identify technical constraints early.

---

## Step 8 — Discuss SEO & Marketing

Review:

- Current rankings
- Google Business Profile
- Existing SEO efforts
- Competitors
- Keywords
- Content strategy
- Marketing goals

Document opportunities for improvement.

---

## Step 9 — Establish Project Expectations

Review:

- Scope
- Timeline
- Communication
- Milestones
- Review process
- Approval process

Ensure everyone has the same expectations.

---

## Step 10 — Questions & Discussion

Provide time for the client to ask questions.

Clarify:

- Process
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Responsibilities

Never leave unanswered questions.

---

## Step 11 — Define Next Steps

Review:

- Action items
- Required information
- Content collection
- Upcoming meetings
- Planning phase

Assign responsibilities for every task.

---

## Step 12 — Document the Meeting

Complete:

- Discovery Notes
- Action Items
- Risks
- Decisions
- Follow-up Tasks

Distribute meeting notes within one business day.

---

# Quality Standards

A successful Discovery Meeting should:

- Build trust
- Clarify expectations
- Identify business goals
- Reveal technical requirements
- Reduce project risk
- Prepare the Planning phase

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Unclear goals | Ask follow-up questions and request examples |
| Missing stakeholders | Schedule a follow-up meeting with decision makers |
| Scope uncertainty | Document assumptions and revisit during Planning |
| Missing assets | Create a content collection plan |
| Unrealistic expectations | Educate the client and adjust the timeline or scope |

---

# Success Criteria

The Discovery Meeting is complete when:

- Business goals are documented
- Target audience is defined
- Technical requirements are understood
- Existing assets are reviewed
- Risks are identified
- Communication expectations are established
- Next steps are assigned

The project is now ready to move into the Planning phase.
