# Standard Operating Procedure: Client Offboarding

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the process for formally transitioning a client after project completion.

The objective is to ensure every client leaves the project with complete confidence, receives all agreed-upon deliverables, understands future support options, and remains a long-term relationship for JS Solutions.

Client Offboarding is not the end of the relationship—it is the beginning of ongoing partnership opportunities.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies whenever a project has been completed and no additional work is currently scheduled.

Examples include:

- Website Development
- Local SEO Engagements
- AI Automation Projects
- Analytics Implementations
- Custom Software Projects

---

# Responsibilities

## Project Manager

Responsible for:

- Coordinating offboarding
- Confirming all obligations have been met
- Delivering final documentation
- Explaining future support options
- Closing internal records

---

## Technical Team

Responsible for:

- Verifying production stability
- Delivering technical documentation
- Confirming client access
- Supporting knowledge transfer

---

## Client

Responsible for:

- Confirming receipt of deliverables
- Reviewing documentation
- Providing final feedback
- Understanding ongoing support options

---

# Estimated Time

Preparation: 30 minutes

Offboarding Meeting: 30–60 minutes

Documentation: 30 minutes

Total: Approximately 1.5–2 hours

---

# Prerequisites

Before beginning Offboarding:

- Project Closeout completed
- Final approval received
- Deliverables accepted
- Outstanding invoices resolved
- Documentation finalized

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Verify Project Completion

Confirm:

- Scope completed
- Deliverables accepted
- Documentation finalized
- Assets delivered

Resolve any remaining issues before proceeding.

---

## Step 2 — Confirm Client Access

Verify the client has access to:

- Website
- Hosting
- Domain registrar
- DNS
- Google Analytics
- Google Search Console
- Google Business Profile
- Source code (if applicable)
- Documentation

Ensure ownership is clear and documented.

---

## Step 3 — Deliver Final Documentation

Provide:

- User guides
- Technical documentation
- Maintenance recommendations
- Login inventory (where appropriate)
- Project summary
- Future recommendations

Document what has been delivered.

---

## Step 4 — Review Support Options

Discuss available services:

- Website Maintenance
- Local SEO
- AI Automation
- Analytics Reporting
- Additional Development
- Consulting

Focus on solving future business needs rather than making a sales pitch.

---

## Step 5 — Schedule Follow-Up

Offer a follow-up review.

Typical timing:

- 30 Days
- 90 Days
- 6 Months

Purpose:

- Review results
- Address questions
- Identify future opportunities

---

## Step 6 — Request Feedback

Ask the client to evaluate:

- Communication
- Professionalism
- Technical quality
- Overall experience

Document suggestions for improving internal processes.

---

## Step 7 — Request Public Review

If appropriate, request:

- Google Review
- LinkedIn Recommendation
- Facebook Recommendation
- Website Testimonial

Only ask after confirming the client is satisfied.

---

## Step 8 — Archive Project Records

Archive:

- Documentation
- Meeting notes
- Communications
- Final deliverables
- Decision logs

Ensure project records remain accessible for future reference.

---

## Step 9 — Update CRM

Update the client record.

Examples:

- Project completed
- Maintenance plan
- Follow-up date
- Future opportunities
- Referral status

---

## Step 10 — Transition Relationship

The client should leave with a clear understanding of:

- Who to contact
- Available support
- Maintenance recommendations
- Future services
- Next scheduled follow-up

The relationship should transition smoothly from active project to long-term partnership.

---

# Quality Standards

Every offboarding process should:

- Feel organized
- Leave no unanswered questions
- Reinforce trust
- Demonstrate professionalism
- Position JS Solutions as a long-term partner

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Client missing access | Restore or transfer ownership before closing |
| Documentation incomplete | Finalize before archiving |
| Outstanding questions | Schedule follow-up meeting |
| Additional work requested | Create a new proposal or maintenance task |
| Future support unclear | Review available service options and response expectations |

---

# Success Criteria

Client Offboarding is complete when:

- Deliverables received
- Client access confirmed
- Documentation delivered
- Feedback collected
- Review requested
- Follow-up scheduled
- CRM updated
- Project archived

The client relationship remains active, even though the project has concluded.

---

# Offboarding Checklist

Before closing the engagement, verify:

- [ ] Client has all required access
- [ ] Final documentation delivered
- [ ] Maintenance options reviewed
- [ ] Feedback collected
- [ ] Public review requested (if appropriate)
- [ ] CRM updated
- [ ] Follow-up scheduled
- [ ] Project archived
