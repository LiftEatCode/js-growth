# Standard Operating Procedure: Proposal Review

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the process for reviewing a client proposal before it is delivered.

The objective is to ensure every proposal accurately reflects the client's goals, aligns with JS Solutions standards, and includes a realistic scope, timeline, and investment.

A thorough review reduces misunderstandings, prevents scope creep, and builds client confidence.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to every proposal prepared by JS Solutions, including:

- Website Development
- Local SEO
- AI Automation
- Analytics
- Custom Software

No proposal should be sent without completing this review.

---

# Responsibilities

## Project Manager

Responsible for:

- Preparing the proposal
- Confirming project scope
- Reviewing timeline
- Coordinating internal approval

---

## Technical Lead

Responsible for:

- Reviewing technical feasibility
- Identifying implementation risks
- Confirming estimated effort
- Verifying proposed solutions

---

## Business Owner

Responsible for:

- Reviewing pricing
- Confirming profitability
- Approving final proposal
- Authorizing delivery to the client

---

# Estimated Time

Preparation: 30–60 minutes

Internal Review: 30 minutes

Final Approval: 15 minutes

Total: Approximately 1–2 hours

---

# Prerequisites

Before beginning the review:

- Discovery Meeting completed
- Discovery Notes documented
- Client Questionnaire reviewed
- Project scope drafted
- Timeline prepared
- Initial proposal completed

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Client Information

Verify:

- Client name
- Business name
- Contact information
- Primary stakeholder
- Proposal number
- Proposal date

Correct any errors before continuing.

---

## Step 2 — Verify Business Goals

Confirm the proposal reflects the client's documented goals.

Review:

- Business objectives
- Success metrics
- Primary challenges
- Target audience

The proposed solution should directly address these goals.

---

## Step 3 — Review Scope of Work

Verify that the proposal clearly defines:

- Included services
- Deliverables
- Project phases
- Client responsibilities
- Exclusions

Avoid vague or ambiguous language.

---

## Step 4 — Review Timeline

Confirm:

- Timeline is realistic
- Milestones are achievable
- Client review periods are included
- Dependencies are documented

Adjust estimates if necessary.

---

## Step 5 — Review Pricing

Verify:

- Pricing matches project scope
- Optional services are clearly identified
- Payment schedule is included
- Discounts (if any) are documented and approved

Ensure the project remains profitable.

---

## Step 6 — Review Technical Requirements

Confirm:

- Recommended technology stack
- Required integrations
- Hosting considerations
- Third-party dependencies
- Technical assumptions

Document any known risks.

---

## Step 7 — Identify Risks

Review for:

- Scope ambiguity
- Missing client assets
- Aggressive timelines
- Technical complexity
- External dependencies

Record mitigation strategies where appropriate.

---

## Step 8 — Proofread the Proposal

Review for:

- Grammar
- Spelling
- Formatting
- Consistency
- Branding
- Accuracy

The proposal should reflect the professionalism of JS Solutions.

---

## Step 9 — Internal Approval

Before sending the proposal, confirm:

- Scope approved
- Timeline approved
- Pricing approved
- Risks acknowledged
- Technical review completed

Record the approval date.

---

## Step 10 — Deliver Proposal

Send the proposal using the agreed communication method.

Include:

- Personalized introduction
- Proposal document
- Instructions for review
- Next steps
- Contact information

Record the delivery date.

---

# Quality Standards

Every proposal should be:

- Clear
- Professional
- Accurate
- Realistic
- Easy to understand
- Aligned with the client's goals

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Scope too broad | Reduce scope or separate into phases |
| Unrealistic timeline | Revise schedule before sending |
| Missing pricing | Update proposal before approval |
| Technical uncertainty | Schedule an internal technical review |
| Client confusion | Clarify language before delivery |

---

# Success Criteria

This SOP is complete when:

- Proposal reviewed
- Scope confirmed
- Timeline approved
- Pricing approved
- Technical review completed
- Internal approval documented
- Proposal delivered to the client
