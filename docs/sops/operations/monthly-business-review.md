# Standard Operating Procedure: Monthly Business Review

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the monthly business review process for JS Solutions.

The objective is to evaluate business performance over the previous month, measure progress toward long-term objectives, identify opportunities for improvement, and establish priorities for the upcoming month.

Monthly reviews provide a higher-level perspective than weekly reviews and support informed business decisions based on measurable results.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to all areas of the business including:

- Clients
- Sales
- Marketing
- Development
- Operations
- Financial performance
- Documentation
- Strategic initiatives

The review should be completed during the final business day of each month.

---

# JS Solutions Standards

Every monthly review should:

- Measure business performance objectively
- Compare results against established goals
- Review operational efficiency
- Evaluate financial performance
- Identify growth opportunities
- Improve documentation and automation
- Establish clear objectives for the next month

---

# Responsibilities

## Business Owner

Responsible for:

- Leading the monthly review
- Evaluating business performance
- Reviewing financial metrics
- Updating business priorities
- Planning the upcoming month

---

# Estimated Time

60–90 minutes

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Monthly Goals

Review the goals established during the previous monthly review.

For each goal determine:

- Completed
- In Progress
- Delayed
- Cancelled

Record lessons learned.

---

## Step 2 — Review Client Portfolio

Review:

- Active clients
- New clients
- Completed projects
- Lost clients
- Client satisfaction
- Maintenance agreements

Identify opportunities to strengthen relationships.

---

## Step 3 — Review Sales Performance

Evaluate:

- New leads
- Discovery meetings
- Proposals sent
- Proposal acceptance rate
- Projects won
- Projects lost

Determine where the sales process can improve.

---

## Step 4 — Review Marketing Performance

Review:

- Blog posts published
- Social media engagement
- Organic search traffic
- Google Business Profile performance
- Newsletter metrics
- Referral activity
- Website traffic

Compare results against previous months.

---

## Step 5 — Review Financial Performance

Review:

- Monthly revenue
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Outstanding invoices
- Expenses
- Profitability
- Cash flow

Identify trends and financial risks.

---

## Step 6 — Review Development

Evaluate:

- Projects completed
- Features delivered
- Bugs resolved
- Deployments
- Technical debt
- Infrastructure improvements

Review overall engineering health.

---

## Step 7 — Review Operations

Evaluate:

- SOP improvements
- Documentation completed
- Internal processes
- Automation created
- Efficiency improvements

Identify repetitive work that can be eliminated.

---

## Step 8 — Review Business Metrics

Review key performance indicators.

Examples:

Business

- Revenue
- Profit
- Active clients
- MRR

Marketing

- Website traffic
- Organic clicks
- Search impressions
- Social engagement

Development

- Features completed
- Deployments
- Bugs resolved

Operations

- SOPs completed
- Playbooks completed
- Automation scripts
- Documentation pages

---

## Step 9 — Identify Opportunities

Ask:

- What worked well?
- What wasted time?
- What should be automated?
- Which services are growing?
- What new opportunities exist?

Document improvement ideas.

---

## Step 10 — Plan Next Month

Define:

- Business goals
- Revenue targets
- Marketing objectives
- Development priorities
- Documentation goals
- Automation initiatives

Assign realistic priorities.

# Quality Standards

A successful monthly review should:

- Measure meaningful business metrics
- Identify trends
- Improve decision-making
- Support long-term planning
- Produce actionable objectives

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Missing business metrics | Improve weekly tracking |
| Unrealistic goals | Set measurable and achievable objectives |
| Declining marketing performance | Review strategy and content quality |
| Revenue fluctuations | Diversify services and recurring income |
| Process inefficiencies | Prioritize automation and documentation |

---

# Success Criteria

The monthly review is successful when:

- Business performance is understood
- KPIs are updated
- Opportunities are documented
- Goals are established
- The next month has a clear plan

---

# Recommended Cadence

**Duration:** 60–90 minutes
