# Standard Operating Procedure: Goal Setting

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the goal-setting framework used by JS Solutions.

The objective is to establish clear, measurable goals that align with the company's long-term vision while providing a structured process for translating strategic objectives into daily execution.

Every goal should contribute directly to sustainable business growth.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to:

- Company goals
- Financial goals
- Marketing goals
- Sales goals
- Development goals
- Operational improvements
- Personal professional development

This process supports planning across annual, quarterly, monthly, weekly, and daily timeframes.

---

# JS Solutions Standards

Every goal should:

- Support the company vision
- Be measurable
- Be realistic
- Have a defined timeline
- Be reviewed regularly
- Have a clear owner
- Be broken into actionable tasks

Goals should create focus—not overwhelm.

---

# Responsibilities

## Business Owner

- Defining strategic goals

# Goal Hierarchy

JS Solutions uses the following planning hierarchy:

```
10-Year Vision

↓

3-Year Objectives

↓

Annual Goals

↓

Quarterly Objectives

↓

Monthly Priorities

↓

Weekly Outcomes

↓

Daily Top Three
```

Every level should directly support the level above it.

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Company Vision

Review:

- Mission
- Vision
- Core Values
- Long-Term Roadmap

Goals should reinforce the desired future of the company.

---

## Step 2 — Define Annual Goals

Examples:

- Annual revenue target
- Recurring revenue target
- Client acquisition target
- Product launches
- Documentation completion
- Website improvements

Limit annual goals to the highest-impact initiatives.

---

## Step 3 — Define Quarterly Objectives

Break annual goals into 90-day objectives.

Each objective should have:

- Expected outcome
- Success metric
- Target completion date

---

## Step 4 — Define Monthly Priorities

For each quarterly objective:

Identify:

- Key milestones
- Deliverables
- Resources required

Avoid overcommitting.

---

## Step 5 — Define Weekly Outcomes

Each week should answer:

"What must be accomplished this week to remain on schedule?"

Weekly outcomes should be measurable.

---

## Step 6 — Define Daily Priorities

Each workday identify:

- Top Priority
- Second Priority
- Third Priority

Completing these three items should represent a successful day.

---

## Step 7 — Measure Progress

Review progress during:

- Weekly Reviews
- Monthly Reviews
- Quarterly Planning
- Annual Planning

Update goals as needed.

---

## Step 8 — Adjust Objectives

When circumstances change:

- Review priorities
- Update timelines
- Reallocate resources
- Remove obsolete goals
- Add new opportunities

Maintain flexibility while protecting long-term direction.

# Quality Standards

Goals should be:

- Specific
- Measurable
- Achievable
- Relevant
- Time-bound

Every goal should produce measurable business value.

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Too many goals | Prioritize the highest-impact objectives |
| Goals not measurable | Define clear KPIs |
| Lack of progress | Break work into smaller milestones |
| Frequent priority changes | Revisit the company vision before changing direction |
| Goals forgotten | Review during every weekly and monthly review |

---

# Success Criteria

Goal setting is successful when:

- Business priorities are clear
- Daily work aligns with long-term vision
- Progress is measurable
- Reviews drive continuous improvement
- Objectives are consistently achieved
