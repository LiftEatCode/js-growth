# Standard Operating Procedure: Daily Startup

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the daily startup routine used at JS Solutions.

The objective is to begin every workday with a clear understanding of priorities, active projects, client needs, and business objectives. A consistent startup routine reduces decision fatigue, improves focus, and ensures that the most important work receives attention first.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to all normal business days and should be followed before beginning client work, development, marketing, or administrative tasks.

---

# JS Solutions Standards

Every workday should begin by:

- Reviewing priorities before checking distractions
- Planning the day before starting execution
- Identifying the highest-value work
- Reviewing active client commitments
- Checking for urgent issues
- Updating task management systems
- Working intentionally rather than reactively

---

# Responsibilities

## Business Owner

Responsible for:

- Reviewing business priorities
- Managing workload
- Monitoring active projects
- Planning the day's schedule
- Addressing urgent client needs

---

# Estimated Time

15–30 minutes

---

# Prerequisites

Before beginning:

- Workspace prepared
- Development environment available
- Calendar accessible
- Task management system available
- Internet connectivity verified

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Calendar

Review today's schedule.

Check:

- Client meetings
- Internal work sessions
- Deadlines
- Follow-up reminders
- Personal commitments

Adjust priorities if necessary.

---

## Step 2 — Review Communications

Check:

- Business email
- Client messages
- Project notifications
- GitHub notifications
- Critical system alerts

Avoid responding immediately unless urgent.

Focus on identifying priorities first.

---

## Step 3 — Review Active Projects

Identify:

- Current milestones
- Blocking issues
- Upcoming deadlines
- Client deliverables

Determine which projects require attention today.

---

## Step 4 — Review Business Dashboard

Review key metrics including:

- New leads
- Active clients
- Website inquiries
- Open proposals
- Current revenue
- Outstanding invoices

Maintain awareness of overall business health.

---

## Step 5 — Review Task List

Organize work into:

High Priority

Medium Priority

Low Priority

Remove outdated tasks and reprioritize as necessary.

---

## Step 6 — Identify Today's Top Three Priorities

Choose the three most important outcomes for the day.

Examples:

- Complete client deliverable
- Finish website feature
- Publish SEO blog
- Complete proposal
- Conduct discovery meeting

Completing these tasks should represent a successful day.

---

## Step 7 — Verify Development Environment

If development work is planned:

Verify:

- Repository synchronized
- Dependencies available
- Local environment operational
- Required credentials accessible

Resolve technical issues before beginning work.

---

## Step 8 — Plan Deep Work Sessions

Reserve uninterrupted focus time.

Examples:

9:00–11:00

Development

11:30–12:30

Documentation

2:00–3:30

Marketing

Protect deep work by minimizing interruptions.

---

## Step 9 — Review Long-Term Goals

Briefly review:

- Weekly objectives
- Monthly goals
- Quarterly priorities
- Annual vision

Ensure today's work contributes toward long-term business growth.

---

## Step 10 — Begin Work

Start with the highest-value task identified during planning.

Avoid switching tasks unnecessarily.

Complete meaningful work before responding to low-priority requests.

---

# Daily Startup Checklist

Before beginning work, verify:

- [ ] Calendar reviewed
- [ ] Communications checked
- [ ] Active projects reviewed
- [ ] Business dashboard reviewed
- [ ] Task list prioritized
- [ ] Top three priorities identified
- [ ] Development environment verified (if applicable)
- [ ] Deep work scheduled
- [ ] Long-term goals reviewed
- [ ] Highest-priority task started

---

# Quality Standards

A successful startup routine should:

- Establish clear priorities
- Reduce distractions
- Improve focus
- Prepare for productive work
- Align daily tasks with business goals

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Too many priorities | Reduce focus to the top three outcomes |
| Reactive work | Plan before responding to communications |
| Frequent interruptions | Schedule protected deep work sessions |
| Missed deadlines | Review upcoming milestones daily |
| Unclear priorities | Review quarterly and weekly objectives |

---

# Success Criteria

The daily startup routine is successful when:

- Priorities are clearly defined
- The day's schedule is organized
- High-value work is identified
- Business objectives are considered
- Work begins with intention rather than reaction
