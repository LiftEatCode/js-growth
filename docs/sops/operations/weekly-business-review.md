# Standard Operating Procedure: Weekly Business Review

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the weekly business review process for JS Solutions.

The objective is to evaluate business performance, measure progress toward strategic goals, identify operational issues, and establish priorities for the upcoming week.

A consistent weekly review ensures the business remains proactive rather than reactive.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to the weekly review of all business operations, including client work, marketing, development, finances, documentation, and long-term planning.

The review should be completed once each week, preferably at the end of the work week.

---

# JS Solutions Standards

Every weekly review should:

- Measure progress objectively
- Review business performance
- Identify obstacles early
- Update priorities
- Track meaningful metrics
- Improve business processes
- End with a clear action plan

---

# Responsibilities

## Business Owner

Responsible for:

- Reviewing business performance
- Updating priorities
- Evaluating ongoing projects
- Identifying improvements
- Planning the upcoming week

---

# Estimated Time

30–60 minutes

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Weekly Goals

Review goals established during the previous week.

Determine:

- Completed
- In Progress
- Delayed
- Cancelled

Record lessons learned.

---

## Step 2 — Review Active Clients

For each active client, review:

- Current status
- Upcoming milestones
- Outstanding requests
- Risks
- Communication needs

Ensure every client has a clear next step.

---

## Step 3 — Review Sales Pipeline

Review:

- New leads
- Discovery meetings
- Open proposals
- Follow-up opportunities
- Closed projects

Identify actions needed to move opportunities forward.

---

## Step 4 — Review Marketing Performance

Evaluate:

- Social media posts published
- Blog articles published
- Website traffic
- Search rankings
- Google Business Profile activity
- Newsletter performance

Identify successful strategies and areas for improvement.

---

## Step 5 — Review Development Progress

Review:

- Active development projects
- Completed milestones
- Bugs resolved
- Technical debt
- Upcoming releases

Ensure projects remain on schedule.

---

## Step 6 — Review Financial Performance

Review:

- Revenue received
- Outstanding invoices
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Project profitability
- Business expenses

Identify any financial concerns.

---

## Step 7 — Review Documentation

Review:

- SOP updates
- Playbook improvements
- Templates created
- Documentation needing revision

Record opportunities to improve company knowledge.

---

## Step 8 — Review Automation Opportunities

Ask:

- What repetitive work occurred this week?
- Can any process be automated?
- Can documentation be improved?
- Can AI assist with this work?

Record ideas for future implementation.

---

## Step 9 — Set Priorities for Next Week

Identify:

- Top three business priorities
- Client priorities
- Development priorities
- Marketing priorities
- Administrative priorities

Assign realistic objectives.

---

## Step 10 — Record Weekly Summary

Document:

- Major accomplishments
- Challenges
- Lessons learned
- Decisions made
- Goals for next week

Maintain a historical record of business progress.

# Quality Standards

A successful weekly review should:

- Provide measurable insights
- Improve decision-making
- Identify issues early
- Align work with long-term goals
- Produce actionable next steps

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Too many priorities | Focus on the highest-impact work |
| Missing metrics | Improve tracking throughout the week |
| Recurring blockers | Address root causes instead of symptoms |
| Incomplete reviews | Schedule dedicated review time on the calendar |
| No action items | End every review with a concrete plan |

---

# Success Criteria

The weekly review is successful when:

- Business performance is understood
- Priorities are updated
- Risks are identified
- Metrics are recorded
- A clear plan exists for the coming week
