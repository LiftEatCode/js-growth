# Standard Operating Procedure: Quarterly Planning

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the quarterly planning process for JS Solutions.

The objective is to evaluate long-term business progress, align operations with the company vision, establish strategic priorities, and allocate resources effectively for the next ninety days.

Quarterly planning bridges the gap between annual goals and daily execution.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to all strategic business planning activities, including:

- Company vision
- Financial planning
- Client growth
- Marketing strategy
- Service development
- Technology initiatives
- Internal operations

Quarterly planning should be completed during the first week of each calendar quarter.

---

# JS Solutions Standards

Every quarterly planning session should:

- Review long-term objectives
- Measure progress objectively
- Focus on business growth
- Prioritize high-impact initiatives
- Eliminate low-value work
- Produce measurable objectives
- End with a clear execution plan

---

# Responsibilities

## Business Owner

Responsible for:

- Leading the planning session
- Reviewing company performance
- Setting strategic objectives
- Prioritizing initiatives
- Allocating business resources

---

# Estimated Time

2–4 hours

---

# Prerequisites

Before beginning:

- Monthly review completed
- Financial reports available
- Marketing reports available
- Development roadmap updated
- Active project list reviewed

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Company Vision

Review:

- Mission
- Vision
- Core values
- Long-term roadmap

Confirm the company remains aligned with its strategic direction.

---

## Step 2 — Review Previous Quarter

Evaluate:

- Goals completed
- Goals delayed
- Goals cancelled
- Major accomplishments
- Significant challenges

Identify lessons learned.

---

## Step 3 — Review Financial Performance

Evaluate:

- Quarterly revenue
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Profitability
- Expenses
- Cash flow
- Revenue by service

Compare results to targets.

---

## Step 4 — Review Client Portfolio

Review:

- Active clients
- New clients
- Client retention
- Client satisfaction
- High-value opportunities
- At-risk relationships

Identify opportunities to strengthen partnerships.

---

## Step 5 — Review Marketing Performance

Evaluate:

- Organic traffic growth
- Search rankings
- Blog performance
- Social media engagement
- Google Business Profile
- Referral sources
- Lead generation

Identify the highest-performing channels.

---

## Step 6 — Review Service Performance

Evaluate each service:

- Website Development
- Local SEO
- AI Automation
- Analytics
- Custom Software

For each service determine:

- Revenue
- Demand
- Profitability
- Improvement opportunities

---

## Step 7 — Review Internal Operations

Evaluate:

- SOP completion
- Playbook progress
- Documentation quality
- Automation improvements
- Internal tools
- Operational efficiency

Identify bottlenecks.

---

## Step 8 — Evaluate Technology

Review:

- Website improvements
- Infrastructure
- Security
- Software updates
- AI initiatives
- Automation roadmap

Plan future technology investments.

---

## Step 9 — Define Quarterly Objectives

Set 3–5 measurable objectives.

Examples:

- Sign three new recurring clients
- Publish twelve SEO blogs
- Complete Local SEO Playbook
- Build Website Audit Tool MVP
- Launch AI Assistant Version 1

Objectives should be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

---

## Step 10 — Create Quarterly Action Plan

Break each objective into:

- Monthly milestones
- Weekly priorities
- Success metrics
- Target completion dates

Assign ownership and expected outcomes.

# Quality Standards

A successful quarterly planning session should:

- Produce measurable objectives
- Align work with company vision
- Prioritize high-value initiatives
- Remove unnecessary work
- Improve operational effectiveness

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Too many objectives | Limit focus to 3–5 strategic goals |
| Unrealistic timelines | Break work into smaller milestones |
| Lack of measurable goals | Define specific KPIs |
| Shifting priorities | Revisit the company vision before planning |
| Poor follow-through | Review progress during weekly and monthly reviews |

---

# Success Criteria

Quarterly planning is successful when:

- Strategic objectives are clearly defined
- Resources are aligned
- Priorities are understood
- KPIs are established
- Execution plans are documented
