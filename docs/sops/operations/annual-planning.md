# Standard Operating Procedure: Annual Planning

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the annual planning process for JS Solutions.

The objective is to evaluate the previous year, establish strategic direction for the upcoming year, allocate resources, define measurable business goals, and ensure every major initiative supports the company's long-term vision.

Annual planning establishes the foundation for quarterly, monthly, weekly, and daily execution.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to all annual business planning activities, including:

- Company strategy
- Financial planning
- Marketing strategy
- Service offerings
- Product development
- Technology investments
- Staffing
- Operations
- Risk management

Annual planning should be completed during the first two weeks of each calendar year.

---

# JS Solutions Standards

Every annual planning session should:

- Begin with the company vision
- Be data-driven
- Review the previous year honestly
- Focus on long-term growth
- Prioritize sustainable improvements
- Produce measurable annual objectives
- Create a roadmap for the year ahead

---

# Responsibilities

## Business Owner

Responsible for:

- Leading annual planning
- Reviewing company performance
- Defining annual objectives
- Approving strategic initiatives
- Allocating resources

---

# Estimated Time

4–8 hours

This may be completed over multiple sessions if needed.

---

# Prerequisites

Before beginning:

- Quarterly review completed
- Financial reports finalized
- Annual analytics available
- Client portfolio reviewed
- Marketing metrics collected
- Development roadmap updated

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Company Vision

Review:

- Mission
- Vision
- Core Values
- Long-Term Roadmap

Confirm that the company's direction remains appropriate.

---

## Step 2 — Review Previous Year

Evaluate:

- Goals achieved
- Goals missed
- Major accomplishments
- Business challenges
- Lessons learned

Identify what should continue, improve, or stop.

---

## Step 3 — Review Financial Performance

Evaluate:

- Annual revenue
- Profit
- Expenses
- Cash flow
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Revenue by service
- Revenue growth

Compare results to previous years.

---

## Step 4 — Review Client Performance

Evaluate:

- Active clients
- New clients
- Lost clients
- Retention rate
- Referral rate
- Client satisfaction

Identify opportunities to improve client relationships.

---

## Step 5 — Review Marketing Performance

Evaluate:

- Organic traffic
- Search rankings
- Blog performance
- Social media growth
- Newsletter performance
- Google Business Profile
- Lead generation
- Website conversions

Document successful strategies.

---

## Step 6 — Review Service Performance

Evaluate each service:

- Website Development
- Local SEO
- AI Automation
- Analytics
- Custom Software

Review:

- Revenue
- Profitability
- Demand
- Customer feedback
- Growth potential

Determine where future investment should be focused.

---

## Step 7 — Review Operations

Evaluate:

- SOP completion
- Playbook progress
- Documentation quality
- Internal tools
- Automation improvements
- Operational efficiency

Identify opportunities to streamline operations.

---

## Step 8 — Review Technology

Evaluate:

- Website improvements
- Infrastructure
- Hosting
- Security
- Development tooling
- AI initiatives
- Automation roadmap

Plan major technology investments.

---

## Step 9 — Establish Annual Objectives

Define 5–10 measurable goals.

Examples:

- Reach $100,000 annual revenue
- Acquire 12 recurring clients
- Launch Website Audit Tool
- Complete all service playbooks
- Release Version 2 of the company website
- Publish 52 SEO blog articles
- Build the JS Solutions Internal CLI

Every objective should be measurable and aligned with the company vision.

---

## Step 10 — Build the Annual Roadmap

Break each annual objective into:

- Quarterly goals
- Monthly milestones
- Success metrics
- Resource requirements
- Target completion dates

Ensure every major initiative has an execution plan.

# Quality Standards

A successful annual planning session should:

- Produce measurable objectives
- Align the business with its long-term vision
- Allocate resources effectively
- Prioritize high-impact initiatives
- Create a practical execution roadmap

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Too many annual goals | Limit objectives to the highest-impact initiatives |
| Goals not measurable | Define KPIs and success metrics |
| Poor follow-through | Break goals into quarterly and monthly milestones |
| Unrealistic expectations | Base planning on historical performance and available resources |
| Shifting priorities | Review the company vision before making major changes |

---

# Success Criteria

Annual planning is successful when:

- Company direction is clear
- Objectives are measurable
- Roadmap is documented
- Resources are allocated
- Quarterly planning can begin immediately
