# Operations Standard Operating Procedures

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This directory contains the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that define how JS Solutions is operated on a daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual basis.

These procedures establish repeatable business routines that support consistent decision-making, strategic planning, operational excellence, and long-term growth.

Operations SOPs are designed to help the business run efficiently regardless of company size.

---

# Philosophy

Successful businesses are built through consistent execution rather than occasional bursts of effort.

Every recurring operational task should follow a documented process.

The objectives of these procedures are to:

- Improve consistency
- Reduce decision fatigue
- Support accountability
- Preserve institutional knowledge
- Enable automation
- Prepare the business for growth

---

# Operations Workflow

The operational management process is divided into the following procedures.

## 1. Daily Startup

Begin each workday by reviewing priorities, communications, projects, and operational health.

---

## 2. Daily Shutdown

End each workday by documenting progress, committing work, planning the next day, and ensuring projects are left in a stable state.

---

## 3. Weekly Business Review

Review operational performance, active projects, financial progress, marketing efforts, and strategic priorities.

---

## 4. Monthly Business Review

Evaluate business performance using operational metrics, financial reporting, marketing analytics, and documentation improvements.

---

## 5. Quarterly Planning

Review long-term objectives, adjust priorities, evaluate business opportunities, and plan the next quarter.

---

## 6. Annual Planning

Define strategic goals, budgeting, service offerings, technology initiatives, hiring plans, and company direction for the coming year.

---

## 7. Goal Setting

Establish, prioritize, monitor, and review business goals at multiple time horizons.

---

## 8. Time Management

Manage work priorities, scheduling, deep work sessions, meetings, and task organization.

---

## 9. Meeting Management

Plan, conduct, document, and follow up on internal and client meetings.

---

## 10. Documentation Review

Maintain the accuracy, quality, and completeness of all company documentation.

---

## 11. Business Continuity

Prepare for operational disruptions by documenting recovery procedures, backup strategies, and critical business assets.

---

# Operating Principles

Operations at JS Solutions should always emphasize:

- Consistency
- Continuous improvement
- Accountability
- Documentation
- Automation
- Security
- Long-term thinking

Whenever repetitive work can be standardized or automated, it should be.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Operations procedures are living documents.

Whenever a better way of operating the business is identified:

- Update the appropriate SOP.
- Record the revision.
- Apply the improvement consistently going forward.
