# Standard Operating Procedure: Blog Publishing

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the process for researching, writing, optimizing, publishing, and monitoring blog content for JS Solutions and its clients.

The objective is to create valuable, search engine optimized content that educates readers, builds authority, supports Local SEO, and generates qualified leads over time.

Every published article should provide genuine value while supporting the client's business goals.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to all blog content created for:

- JS Solutions
- Website Development clients
- Local SEO clients
- AI Automation clients
- Analytics clients
- Custom Software clients

---

# Responsibilities

## Marketing Manager

Responsible for:

- Approving blog topics
- Reviewing SEO strategy
- Monitoring performance
- Maintaining the editorial calendar

---

## Content Writer

Responsible for:

- Keyword research
- Content creation
- Internal linking
- Metadata optimization
- Proofreading

---

## Designer / AI Assistant

Responsible for:

- Creating featured images
- Producing supporting graphics
- Maintaining visual consistency
- Optimizing image sizes

---

# Estimated Time

Keyword Research: 30–60 minutes

Outline Creation: 30 minutes

Writing: 2–4 hours

SEO Optimization: 30 minutes

Publishing: 20 minutes

Quality Review: 20 minutes

Total: Approximately 4–7 hours per article

---

# Prerequisites

Before writing:

- Monthly content calendar approved
- Target keyword selected
- Search intent identified
- Target audience defined

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Select the Target Keyword

Choose one primary keyword.

Identify:

- Search volume
- Competition
- Search intent
- Local relevance
- Business value

Select supporting keywords where appropriate.

---

## Step 2 — Research the Topic

Review:

- Frequently asked customer questions
- Existing website content
- Competitor articles
- Industry resources
- Seasonal opportunities

Focus on providing practical value.

---

## Step 3 — Create an Outline

Develop a logical structure.

Typical outline:

- Introduction
- Main sections
- Frequently asked questions
- Summary
- Call-to-action

Ensure headings are descriptive and organized.

---

## Step 4 — Write the Article

Create original content that:

- Solves a problem
- Answers common questions
- Demonstrates expertise
- Matches search intent

Write in a professional, approachable tone.

Avoid keyword stuffing.

---

## Step 5 — Optimize for SEO

Review:

- Title tag
- Meta description
- H1 heading
- H2/H3 headings
- Internal links
- External references (when appropriate)
- Image alt text
- URL slug

Ensure the primary keyword is used naturally.

---

## Step 6 — Create Supporting Images

Prepare:

- Featured image
- Supporting graphics
- Infographics
- Branded illustrations

Verify:

- Branding consistency
- Mobile readability
- Optimized file size

---

## Step 7 — Review the Article

Verify:

- Grammar
- Spelling
- Formatting
- Accuracy
- SEO optimization
- Readability
- Internal links
- Calls-to-action

Complete a final editorial review before publishing.

---

## Step 8 — Publish the Article

Confirm:

- Category
- Tags
- Author
- Publish date
- Featured image
- Metadata
- URL slug

Publish according to the editorial calendar.

---

## Step 9 — Promote the Article

Share through:

- Facebook
- LinkedIn
- Google Business Profile
- Email newsletter
- Other relevant platforms

Repurpose the content into additional marketing assets where appropriate.

---

## Step 10 — Monitor Performance

Review:

- Organic traffic
- Search rankings
- Click-through rate
- Average engagement time
- Bounce rate
- Leads generated
- Conversions

Record insights for future content planning.

---

# Content Standards

Every article should:

- Solve a real problem
- Demonstrate expertise
- Be easy to read
- Include a clear CTA
- Support SEO objectives
- Reflect JS Solutions branding

Quality always takes priority over quantity.

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Weak keyword selection | Revisit keyword research before writing |
| Thin content | Expand with practical examples and FAQs |
| Poor readability | Simplify language and shorten paragraphs |
| Missing internal links | Link to relevant service pages and blogs |
| Weak CTA | Add a clear next step for readers |

---

# Success Criteria

A blog is considered complete when:

- Target keyword optimized
- SEO review completed
- Graphics added
- Internal links verified
- CTA included
- Published successfully
- Promotion scheduled
