# Standard Operating Procedure: Google Business Profile Management

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the process for managing and optimizing Google Business Profiles (GBP) for JS Solutions and its clients.

The objective is to improve local search visibility, increase customer engagement, generate qualified leads, and maintain accurate business information across Google Search and Google Maps.

A properly maintained Google Business Profile is one of the most valuable Local SEO assets for any local business.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to:

- JS Solutions
- Website clients
- Local SEO clients
- Businesses with physical locations
- Service-area businesses

---

# Responsibilities

## Marketing Manager

Responsible for:

- Reviewing profile performance
- Approving monthly updates
- Monitoring Local SEO metrics
- Managing review strategy

---

## Local SEO Specialist

Responsible for:

- Optimizing the profile
- Publishing posts
- Updating services
- Monitoring insights
- Managing photos

---

## Client

Responsible for:

- Providing updated business information
- Responding to operational changes
- Assisting with customer review requests
- Approving major profile changes

---

# Estimated Time

Profile Review: 20 minutes

Content Creation: 20–30 minutes

Photo Updates: 15 minutes

Performance Review: 20 minutes

Total: Approximately 1–2 hours per month

---

# Prerequisites

Before updating the profile:

- Business ownership verified
- Google Business Profile access confirmed
- Branding guidelines available
- Current business information verified

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Verify Business Information

Review:

- Business name
- Address
- Phone number
- Website
- Hours of operation
- Holiday hours
- Service areas

Ensure all information is accurate and consistent with the website.

---

## Step 2 — Review Business Categories

Verify:

- Primary category
- Secondary categories

Update categories only when they better reflect the client's services.

---

## Step 3 — Update Services

Review:

- Services
- Service descriptions
- Products (if applicable)
- Pricing (if displayed)

Ensure offerings match the current website.

---

## Step 4 — Publish a Google Post

Create a post that supports one of the following:

- Educational content
- Promotions
- Seasonal tips
- New services
- Company updates
- Community involvement
- Recent projects

Include:

- Branded image
- Clear call-to-action
- Relevant landing page (if applicable)

---

## Step 5 — Upload New Photos

Add recent images such as:

- Completed projects
- Team photos
- Facility photos
- Vehicles
- Equipment
- Products

Use high-quality, authentic images whenever possible.

---

## Step 6 — Monitor Reviews

Review new customer feedback.

For each review:

- Respond professionally
- Thank positive reviewers
- Address concerns respectfully
- Escalate serious issues internally if needed

Aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

---

## Step 7 — Review Questions & Answers

Check the Questions & Answers section.

Answer unanswered questions promptly.

Add common questions proactively when appropriate.

---

## Step 8 — Review Insights

Analyze:

- Searches
- Views
- Website clicks
- Phone calls
- Direction requests
- Photo views

Compare results to previous reporting periods.

---

## Step 9 — Identify Opportunities

Look for opportunities such as:

- Additional service categories
- Missing photos
- New FAQs
- Seasonal promotions
- New customer reviews
- Content ideas

Record recommendations.

---

## Step 10 — Document Monthly Activity

Record:

- Updates completed
- New posts
- Photos uploaded
- Reviews received
- Performance metrics
- Recommended next steps

Store documentation with the client's monthly marketing records.

---

# Quality Standards

Every Google Business Profile should:

- Be fully completed
- Match website information
- Display current photos
- Receive regular updates
- Include timely review responses
- Support Local SEO goals

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Incorrect business information | Update immediately across all platforms |
| Few customer reviews | Implement a review request strategy |
| Outdated photos | Schedule monthly photo updates |
| Low engagement | Increase posting frequency and improve CTAs |
| Category mismatch | Review and update categories based on services |

---

# Success Criteria

Google Business Profile management is successful when:

- Business information is accurate
- Monthly posts published
- Photos updated
- Reviews monitored
- Questions answered
- Performance documented
- Recommendations identified
