# Marketing Standard Operating Procedures

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This directory contains the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that define how JS Solutions plans, creates, publishes, and evaluates marketing activities.

Marketing is a continuous process that supports brand awareness, lead generation, customer engagement, and long-term business growth.

These SOPs establish repeatable workflows that ensure consistent messaging, professional execution, and measurable results across every marketing channel.

---

# Philosophy

Marketing should never rely on inspiration alone.

Successful marketing is built on:

- Consistency
- Planning
- Quality
- Measurement
- Continuous improvement

Every marketing activity should support a defined business objective and reinforce the JS Solutions brand.

---

# Marketing Workflow

The marketing process is divided into five core procedures.

## 1. Social Media Posting

**Purpose**

Plan, create, review, publish, and monitor content across social media platforms while maintaining a consistent brand voice.

---

## 2. Monthly Content Planning

**Purpose**

Develop a structured content calendar that aligns with business goals, seasonal opportunities, and target audiences.

---

## 3. Blog Publishing

**Purpose**

Create high-quality, SEO-focused articles that educate customers, build authority, and improve search visibility.

---

## 4. Google Business Profile Management

**Purpose**

Maintain an optimized Google Business Profile through regular updates, posts, photos, review management, and performance monitoring.

---

## 5. Newsletter Management

**Purpose**

Plan, create, send, and evaluate email newsletters that strengthen client relationships and promote services.

---

# Marketing Objectives

Every marketing activity should support one or more of the following objectives:

- Increase brand awareness
- Generate qualified leads
- Improve search visibility
- Build authority
- Strengthen customer relationships
- Increase website traffic
- Support long-term business growth

---

# Operating Principles

Marketing at JS Solutions should always be:

- Consistent
- Educational
- Authentic
- Professional
- Data-driven
- Customer-focused

Every piece of content should provide value while reinforcing the company's expertise and credibility.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Marketing procedures are living documents.

Performance should be reviewed regularly using analytics, engagement metrics, and client feedback.

Insights gained from completed campaigns should be incorporated into future planning and execution.
