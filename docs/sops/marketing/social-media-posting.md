# Standard Operating Procedure: Social Media Posting

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the process for planning, creating, reviewing, publishing, and monitoring social media content for JS Solutions and its clients.

The objective is to maintain a consistent brand presence, educate audiences, increase engagement, generate qualified leads, and support long-term marketing goals.

Every published post should provide value while reinforcing the client's brand and business objectives.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to all social media content created by JS Solutions.

Platforms may include:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- X (Twitter)
- Threads
- TikTok
- YouTube Community Posts

---

# Responsibilities

## Marketing Manager

Responsible for:

- Planning content
- Approving content calendar
- Reviewing branding
- Monitoring campaign performance

---

## Content Creator

Responsible for:

- Writing captions
- Creating graphics
- Selecting hashtags
- Preparing scheduled posts

---

## Designer / AI Assistant

Responsible for:

- Creating branded graphics
- Maintaining visual consistency
- Following branding guidelines
- Exporting optimized images

---

# Estimated Time

Research: 15–30 minutes

Writing: 20–30 minutes

Graphic Creation: 20–40 minutes

Review: 10 minutes

Scheduling: 10 minutes

Total: Approximately 1–2 hours per post

---

# Prerequisites

Before creating content:

- Monthly content calendar approved
- Branding guidelines available
- Target audience identified
- Campaign objective defined

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Define the Objective

Every post should have one primary objective.

Examples:

- Brand Awareness
- Education
- Lead Generation
- Engagement
- Promotion
- Community Building
- Customer Appreciation

Avoid creating content without a defined purpose.

---

## Step 2 — Select the Topic

Choose a topic based on:

- Content calendar
- Seasonal opportunities
- Frequently asked questions
- Customer pain points
- Industry trends
- Company announcements
- Recent projects

Whenever possible, educate before promoting.

---

## Step 3 — Identify the Target Audience

Determine who the post is intended to reach.

Examples:

- Business owners
- Homeowners
- Fleet managers
- Local customers
- Existing clients
- Prospective clients

Tailor the messaging to that audience.

---

## Step 4 — Write the Caption

The caption should:

- Capture attention
- Provide value
- Encourage engagement
- Include a clear call-to-action

Whenever appropriate, include:

- Questions
- Tips
- Stories
- Educational insights

Maintain the JS Solutions brand voice:

- Professional
- Helpful
- Confident
- Approachable

---

## Step 5 — Create Supporting Graphics

Design supporting visuals.

Examples include:

- Branded graphics
- Infographics
- Before-and-after images
- Project photos
- AI-generated artwork
- Carousels
- Promotional banners

Verify:

- Logo placement
- Brand colors
- Typography
- Image quality
- Mobile readability

---

## Step 6 — Review Branding

Before publishing, confirm:

- Correct logo
- Correct phone number
- Correct website
- Consistent colors
- Professional appearance

All marketing materials should reinforce the brand.

---

## Step 7 — Add Calls-to-Action

Examples:

- Learn More
- Visit Our Website
- Request a Quote
- Contact Us
- Schedule a Consultation
- Leave a Comment
- Share Your Experience

Every post should encourage the audience to take an action.

---

## Step 8 — Add Hashtags

Use relevant hashtags.

Guidelines:

- Brand hashtags
- Industry hashtags
- Local hashtags
- Campaign hashtags

Avoid excessive or irrelevant hashtags.

---

## Step 9 — Publish or Schedule

Verify:

- Correct platform
- Correct publish date
- Correct time
- Correct audience

Whenever possible, schedule content in advance.

---

## Step 10 — Monitor Performance

Review:

- Reach
- Impressions
- Engagement
- Comments
- Shares
- Saves
- Link clicks
- Leads generated

Respond to comments and messages promptly.

Document significant performance insights.

---

# Quality Standards

Every social media post should:

- Reflect the brand
- Provide value
- Be visually appealing
- Include a clear objective
- Include a call-to-action
- Be free of spelling and grammar errors

Consistency is more important than frequency.

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Low engagement | Test different content types and posting times |
| Weak visuals | Improve image quality and branding |
| No clear CTA | Add one before publishing |
| Off-brand messaging | Review Branding Guide |
| Incorrect information | Verify all details before publishing |

---

# Success Criteria

A successful post:

- Meets its intended objective
- Maintains brand consistency
- Encourages audience interaction
- Supports broader marketing goals
- Is documented for future performance analysis

---

# Social Media Publishing Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

- [ ] Objective defined
- [ ] Target audience identified
- [ ] Caption reviewed
- [ ] Graphic approved
- [ ] Branding verified
- [ ] CTA included
- [ ] Hashtags added
- [ ] Platform selected
- [ ] Publish time confirmed
- [ ] Final review completed
