# Standard Operating Procedure: Newsletter Management

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the process for planning, creating, reviewing, sending, and evaluating email newsletters for JS Solutions and its clients.

The objective is to build stronger customer relationships, increase brand awareness, drive website traffic, generate qualified leads, and support long-term business growth through consistent email communication.

Every newsletter should educate, inform, or provide value to subscribers.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to:

- JS Solutions
- Website clients
- Local SEO clients
- Marketing clients
- Existing customer communications
- Prospect email campaigns

---

# Responsibilities

## Marketing Manager

Responsible for:

- Planning newsletter campaigns
- Approving email content
- Reviewing campaign performance
- Maintaining the email calendar

---

## Content Creator

Responsible for:

- Writing newsletter content
- Preparing calls-to-action
- Proofreading
- Coordinating supporting content

---

## Designer / AI Assistant

Responsible for:

- Creating email graphics
- Optimizing images
- Maintaining branding consistency
- Preparing visual assets

---

# Estimated Time

Planning: 30 minutes

Writing: 1–2 hours

Design: 30–60 minutes

Review: 20 minutes

Testing: 20 minutes

Scheduling: 15 minutes

Total: Approximately 3–5 hours per newsletter

---

# Prerequisites

Before creating a newsletter:

- Campaign objective defined
- Target audience identified
- Content approved
- Email list verified
- Branding guidelines available

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Define the Campaign Objective

Every newsletter should have one primary objective.

Examples:

- Educate customers
- Promote a service
- Announce a product
- Share company news
- Drive website traffic
- Generate consultations
- Increase engagement

Avoid combining too many objectives into a single email.

---

## Step 2 — Identify the Audience

Determine who should receive the newsletter.

Examples:

- Existing clients
- Prospective clients
- Previous customers
- Website subscribers
- Industry-specific contacts

Tailor messaging to the audience.

---

## Step 3 — Create the Content

Prepare:

- Subject line
- Preview text
- Introduction
- Main content
- Call-to-action
- Closing message

Write concise, valuable content.

---

## Step 4 — Prepare Supporting Assets

Create or collect:

- Featured image
- Supporting graphics
- Icons
- Buttons
- Links
- Landing pages

Optimize all images for email delivery.

---

## Step 5 — Build the Email

Using the approved email platform:

Verify:

- Branding
- Layout
- Mobile responsiveness
- Links
- Images
- Buttons

Maintain a clean and professional appearance.

---

## Step 6 — Review the Newsletter

Verify:

- Grammar
- Spelling
- Branding
- Formatting
- Links
- Contact information
- Call-to-action

Perform both desktop and mobile reviews.

---

## Step 7 — Send Test Emails

Send test emails to internal reviewers.

Verify:

- Layout
- Deliverability
- Images
- Links
- Personalization
- Spam score (if available)

Correct any issues before scheduling.

---

## Step 8 — Schedule or Send

Confirm:

- Recipient list
- Send date
- Send time
- Campaign name
- Tracking enabled

Schedule according to the marketing calendar.

---

## Step 9 — Monitor Campaign Performance

Review:

- Delivery rate
- Open rate
- Click-through rate
- Unsubscribe rate
- Bounce rate
- Replies
- Conversions

Document campaign results.

---

## Step 10 — Record Lessons Learned

After each campaign, document:

- What performed well
- Areas for improvement
- High-performing subject lines
- Successful calls-to-action
- Audience feedback
- Ideas for future newsletters

Use these insights to improve future campaigns.

---

# Quality Standards

Every newsletter should:

- Provide value
- Match the brand
- Be mobile-friendly
- Include a clear CTA
- Contain accurate information
- Follow email marketing best practices

Subscribers should feel the email was worth opening.

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Low open rate | Test stronger subject lines and send times |
| Low click rate | Improve CTA placement and relevance |
| Broken links | Test all links before sending |
| Poor mobile layout | Review responsive formatting |
| High unsubscribe rate | Increase content value and audience targeting |

---

# Success Criteria

Newsletter management is successful when:

- Campaign objective achieved
- Email delivered successfully
- Performance metrics recorded
- Audience engagement measured
- Lessons documented for future improvement

---

# Newsletter Checklist

Before sending, verify:

- [ ] Objective defined
- [ ] Audience confirmed
- [ ] Subject line reviewed
- [ ] Content approved
- [ ] Images optimized
- [ ] Branding verified
- [ ] Links tested
- [ ] Test email reviewed
- [ ] Schedule confirmed
- [ ] Tracking enabled

---

# KPIs to Track

Review after every campaign:

- Delivery Rate
- Open Rate
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- Click-to-Open Rate (CTOR)
- Bounce Rate
- Unsubscribe Rate
- Spam Complaints
- Website Traffic
- Leads Generated
- Conversions

Track trends over time rather than focusing on a single campaign.

---

# Platform Standards

JS Solutions currently uses:

- Resend for email delivery
- Custom branded templates
- Analytics tracking
- Website landing pages for campaign destinations

Platform-specific implementation may change over time, but the workflow defined in this SOP should remain consistent.
