# Standard Operating Procedure: Monthly Content Planning

> **Part of the JS Solutions Standard Operating Procedures**

---

# Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the process for planning monthly marketing content for JS Solutions and its clients.

The objective is to create a balanced, strategic content calendar that supports business goals, maintains consistent brand visibility, improves SEO, and encourages audience engagement.

Planning content in advance reduces last-minute work and creates a more intentional marketing strategy.

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to all recurring content planning activities for:

- JS Solutions
- Website clients
- Local SEO clients
- Social media management clients
- Google Business Profile management

---

# Responsibilities

## Marketing Manager

Responsible for:

- Creating the monthly content calendar
- Aligning content with business goals
- Reviewing campaign performance
- Approving monthly plans

---

## Content Creator

Responsible for:

- Researching topics
- Writing captions
- Drafting blog ideas
- Preparing supporting content

---

## Designer / AI Assistant

Responsible for:

- Creating graphics
- Generating illustrations
- Maintaining brand consistency
- Preparing visual assets

---

# Estimated Time

Research: 1–2 hours

Planning: 2–3 hours

Content Calendar Creation: 1 hour

Review: 30 minutes

Total: Approximately 4–6 hours per month

---

# Prerequisites

Before planning content:

- Previous month's analytics reviewed
- Business goals updated
- Seasonal events identified
- Promotions confirmed
- Campaign priorities established

---

# Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Previous Month

Analyze:

- Reach
- Engagement
- Clicks
- Website traffic
- Leads generated
- Top-performing posts
- Lowest-performing posts

Identify trends before planning new content.

---

## Step 2 — Review Business Objectives

Determine priorities for the upcoming month.

Examples:

- Generate leads
- Increase website traffic
- Promote a service
- Build brand awareness
- Educate customers
- Increase local visibility

Every planned post should support at least one objective.

---

## Step 3 — Identify Monthly Themes

Select one or more themes.

Examples:

- Seasonal maintenance
- Industry education
- Customer success stories
- Community involvement
- Frequently asked questions
- Promotions
- Team highlights
- Behind-the-scenes content

Themes create consistency throughout the month.

---

## Step 4 — Research Content Ideas

Gather ideas from:

- Customer questions
- Sales conversations
- Google Search Console
- Keyword research
- Industry trends
- Seasonal events
- Competitor research
- Previous successful posts

Record all ideas for future use.

---

## Step 5 — Build the Content Calendar

Distribute content across the month.

Maintain a balanced mix of:

- Educational posts
- Promotional posts
- Community engagement
- Testimonials
- Behind-the-scenes
- Tips
- FAQs
- Blog promotion
- Polls
- Company updates

Avoid publishing similar content on consecutive days.

---

## Step 6 — Coordinate Supporting Content

Identify supporting materials needed.

Examples:

- Blog articles
- Graphics
- Videos
- AI-generated artwork
- Customer photos
- Infographics
- Landing pages

Begin production early.

---

## Step 7 — Schedule Production

Assign deadlines for:

- Writing
- Design
- Reviews
- Approval
- Publishing

Leave time for revisions.

---

## Step 8 — Review Calendar

Verify:

- Balanced content mix
- Branding consistency
- Seasonal relevance
- Campaign alignment
- Posting frequency

Adjust before approval.

---

## Step 9 — Obtain Approval

Review the content calendar internally.

Confirm:

- Topics approved
- Promotions verified
- Branding consistent
- Publishing schedule finalized

Document approval.

---

## Step 10 — Publish Planning Documents

Finalize:

- Monthly calendar
- Production schedule
- Asset checklist
- Campaign objectives

Distribute to the team.

---

# Recommended Monthly Content Mix

Aim for approximately:

- 40% Educational
- 20% Engagement
- 20% Promotional
- 10% Social Proof
- 10% Company Culture

Adjust based on client goals and campaign performance.

---

# Quality Standards

Every monthly plan should:

- Support business objectives
- Maintain branding
- Balance content types
- Include clear calls-to-action
- Provide value to the audience
- Be realistic to execute

Consistency is more valuable than volume.

---

# Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|--------|------------|
| Too much promotional content | Increase educational and engagement posts |
| Running out of ideas | Review FAQs, analytics, and customer conversations |
| Missed deadlines | Build content further in advance |
| Seasonal conflicts | Review annual marketing calendar |
| Uneven posting schedule | Rebalance the content calendar |

---

# Success Criteria

Monthly planning is complete when:

- Content calendar approved
- Topics assigned
- Production schedule created
- Assets identified
- Objectives documented
- Publishing schedule finalized

---

# Monthly Planning Checklist

Before the month begins, verify:

- [ ] Previous month reviewed
- [ ] Business goals updated
- [ ] Monthly themes selected
- [ ] Topics researched
- [ ] Calendar completed
- [ ] Assets identified
- [ ] Production scheduled
- [ ] Calendar approved

---

# KPIs to Track

Review monthly performance:

- Website traffic
- Reach
- Impressions
- Engagement rate
- Click-through rate
- Shares
- Comments
- Leads generated
- Conversion rate
- Follower growth

Use insights to improve next month's content plan.
