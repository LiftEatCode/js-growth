# JS Solutions Master Roadmap

> **The strategic roadmap for JS Solutions.**
>
> This document serves as the single source of truth for the long-term direction of the business. It defines the vision, strategic priorities, major initiatives, and roadmap for building JS Solutions into a scalable technology company.

---

# Mission

Help local businesses grow through technology, automation, AI, software, and data-driven marketing while building proprietary systems that improve both our business and our clients' businesses.

---

# Vision

Build a technology company that combines exceptional digital services with powerful software products.

JS Solutions is more than a web agency.

Our goal is to build a documented, automated, AI-powered business that scales through systems instead of headcount.

Every improvement we make internally has the potential to become a product that creates value for clients and recurring revenue for the company.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every repeatable process should follow the same lifecycle.

```text
Learn

↓

Document

↓

Standardize

↓

Automate

↓

Delegate

↓

Scale
```

This philosophy applies to every service, workflow, and internal process.

# Strategic Pillars

## Business

Build a profitable, scalable technology company.

---

## Services

Deliver world-class digital services with consistent processes.

Current services include:

- Website Development
- Local SEO
- AI Automation
- Business Automation
- Analytics & Reporting

Future services will be added as the business grows.

---

## Documentation

Document every repeatable process.

The documentation repository is the operational knowledge base of the company.

Documentation enables:

- Consistency
- Training
- Delegation
- Automation
- AI integration

---

## Automation

Every repetitive task should eventually be automated.

Automation reduces manual work, improves consistency, and increases capacity.

---

## Artificial Intelligence

Leverage AI to improve:

- Sales
- Marketing
- Development
- Operations
- Customer Support
- Internal Knowledge

AI should enhance—not replace—human expertise.

---

## Products

Develop proprietary software that grows out of internal tools and client needs.

Long-term recurring revenue should come from products as well as services.
